The Palma newspaper Diario de Mallorca has launched *Diario de Negocios*, a new section of its website dedicated to the economy and business in the Balearic Islands.

## What it offers

The new space will publish:

- interviews with business leaders;
- reports on the island economy;
- coverage of business events;
- news about companies and sectors.

## The columnists

The section will also include analysis from professionals in the business world, who will offer their views on economic issues affecting the islands. Among them are Fausto Oviedo, Jaume Pujol, Arantxa Vico, Jorge Morell, Domènec Biosca, Sebastián Lora, Robert Casajuana and Alberto Vergés.

## Why business news matters in the Balearics

The Balearic economy is one of the most dynamic in Spain, but also one of the most dependent on a single sector. Tourism drives growth and employment, while issues such as housing, labour shortages, energy, water and the cost of living shape the debate about the islands' future. Local business coverage helps residents, entrepreneurs and investors understand these trends and how they affect them.

## A changing media landscape

The launch reflects a broader trend among regional newspapers, which are creating specialised sections and products to attract readers and advertisers as they adapt to the digital market. Business news, with a loyal and professional audience, has become one of the areas in which local media are investing.

For readers interested in the islands' economy, the new section adds another source of information and analysis to the Balearic media landscape.
