## Introduction to Changes
The Eivissa Town Council has approved several changes in its latest ordinary meeting, including updates to the waste tax and rent aid rules. According to the council, the new waste tax order for 2027 will modify the criteria for distributing the costs of the service among users and adapt to the requirements of the Waste and Contaminated Soil Law for a circular economy.

## Waste Tax Changes
The new waste tax order introduces a system based on the cadastral value of homes to calculate the domestic quota, with eight different trams. This system replaces the previous one, which was based on the surface area of properties. The domestic quotas will range from 79.82 to 333.80 euros per year. The council said that 4,285 properties will see their quota reduced, with a 6.1% decrease from the current 85 euros to 79.82 euros. A 90% reduction will be applied to certain pensioners and people with disabilities, depending on their family income. Additionally, a 5% to 20% reduction will be applied to homes that make at least 15 deliveries to municipal waste collection points.

## Rent Aid Changes
The council has also modified the rules for municipal rent aid, introducing a non-competitive concurrence system based on verifying the requirements. Applicants will need to prove they have paid their rent during the application process, and the aid can be paid directly into their bank account once approved. The aid can cover up to 70% of the accredited rent expense, within the limits set by each call. The council will establish different lines of aid depending on the characteristics of the families and age of the applicants.

## Urban Planning and Mobility
The council has approved the initial modification of the General Urban Planning Plan (PGOU) to transform 3,273 square meters of municipal land in Es Llimoners-Sa Real into a public green area. The project is related to a neighborhood participation process that concluded in June 2026, in which residents expressed their preference for a traditional green area with vegetation and spaces for walking and socializing. The council has also signed an agreement with the Insular Council of Eivissa to finance the opening of new roads and intersections around the Sant Josep avenue, with a budget of 961,499.55 euros. Furthermore, the council has initiated a procedure to increase the number of municipal taxi licenses by 13, including six for vehicles adapted for people with reduced mobility.
