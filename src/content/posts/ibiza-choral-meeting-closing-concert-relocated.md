## New Venue for Closing Concert
The Consell Insular d'Eivissa has announced that the closing concert of the II Trobada de Corals Lina Bufí, initially scheduled to take place at the Santo Domingo church, will now be held at the Santa Creu church in Vila. The concert, featuring the Cor Amics de sa Música, will take place this Sunday, 15 November, at 20:30, half an hour later than originally announced.

## About the Event
The II Trobada de Corals Lina Bufí has brought together 19 choral groups from across the island for a series of six concerts held in churches in all five municipalities. The event, revived this year by the Consell d'Eivissa, pays tribute to the figure of Lina Bufí and aims to promote the island's choral music scene, bring music to the community, and strengthen the link between culture, heritage, and community.

## Closing Concert Details
The closing concert will be performed by the Cor Amics de sa Música, directed by Nèlida Boned Bufí, and will also serve to commemorate the 50th anniversary of the group, one of the longest-running choral ensembles in Ibiza. Admission to the concert will be free until the venue is full.

## Background
The Consell d'Eivissa, the island council of Ibiza, is responsible for promoting and preserving the island's cultural heritage. The Trobada de Corals Lina Bufí is one of the council's initiatives to support and showcase the island's choral music scene. The event is a celebration of the island's rich cultural traditions and provides an opportunity for residents and visitors to enjoy live music in unique and historic settings.
