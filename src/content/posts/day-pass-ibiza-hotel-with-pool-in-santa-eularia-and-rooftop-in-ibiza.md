Meliá Ibiza, in Santa Eulària des Riu, offers a day pass that allows visitors who are not staying at the hotel to spend the day by its pools, with a Balinese bed and credit to spend on food, drinks or the spa.

## Two options

**Main pool Balinese bed**

- An ice bucket with two bottles of water.
- Towels.
- €100 of credit for food, drinks or spa treatments.

This option is next to the main pool, for those who want to stay close to the water in a relaxed atmosphere.

**Rooftop Balinese bed**

- An ice bucket with a bottle of cava.
- Sun cream.
- Towels.
- €150 of credit.

The rooftop offers views over Santa Eulària and the Mediterranean and a more exclusive setting.

## LLUM rooftop

The hotel's rooftop bar, LLUM, is open to the public in the evenings and is known for its views at sunset over the bay.

## Why day passes are popular

Day passes have become popular in Ibiza and across the Balearics, both with residents who want a day of relaxation without booking a room, and with visitors staying in rental apartments or villas without a pool. Hotels see them as a way to make use of their facilities and attract customers to their bars, restaurants and spas.

## Tips

- Book in advance, especially at weekends and in August, as the number of beds is limited.
- Check what time you can arrive and must leave.
- Confirm whether the credit can be used for food, drinks and spa treatments, and whether any unused credit is lost at the end of the day.

Prices and conditions vary through the season, so it is worth checking the hotel's website before booking.
