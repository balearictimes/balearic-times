The Spanish actress Aurora Sánchez, known to television audiences for her roles in *Ana y los 7* and *Acacias 38*, has died at the age of 66. Her death was announced by the actors' union, the Unión de Actores y Actrices.

## A familiar face on television

Born in Huelva, Sánchez built a career spanning four decades in television, film and theatre. She became widely known for her role in TVE's hit series *Ana y los 7* (2002–2005), the family comedy starring Ana Obregón, and later for her character Paciencia in the long-running period drama *Acacias 38*, in which she appeared in hundreds of episodes.

She also worked on series including *Policías, en el corazón de la calle* and *El botones Sacarino*.

## Later work

She remained active in recent years, appearing in the Prime Video romantic comedy series *Pequeñas coincidencias* and in feature films.

## The stage

Although television made her known to the wider public, the theatre was her first love. Her stage work included Federico García Lorca's *La casa de Bernarda Alba* and *Yerma*, contemporary plays such as *Arizona* and *Cancún*, and *Picasso andaluz o la muerte del Minotauro*.

## Tributes

The actors' union said she had left a deep mark on colleagues and audiences. Former co-stars shared messages on social media remembering her professionalism, warmth and generosity towards younger actors.

## A character actress

Sánchez belonged to a generation of Spanish character actors whose supporting roles gave life to some of the country's most popular television series. Her ability to move between comedy and drama made her a valued presence on set and on stage.

Her family has asked for privacy.
