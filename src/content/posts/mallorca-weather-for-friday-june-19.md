Mallorca is entering the first heatwave of the summer. Aemet has issued yellow warnings for Friday and an amber warning for the south of the island on Saturday, with sunshine, heat and some African dust in the air.

## Friday: yellow warning

A yellow warning is in force from midday to 8pm for the south, the interior and the north and north-east of Mallorca, with highs of around 37°C.

| Town | Min | Max |
|---|---|---|
| Alcúdia | 20°C | 31°C |
| Andratx | 20°C | 32°C |
| Binissalem | 18°C | 38°C |
| Palma | 20°C | 36°C |
| Pollença | 20°C | 34°C |
| Sant Llorenç | 19°C | 32°C |
| Santanyí | 19°C | 32°C |
| Sineu | 20°C | 35°C |
| Sóller | 18°C | 37°C |

Breezes will be mainly from the south-east and east, moderate at times on the coast.

## Saturday: amber in the south

On Saturday, the south of the island moves to an amber warning, with highs of up to 39°C. The rest of Mallorca, except the east, remains on yellow. It will be breezy, with dust still in the atmosphere and a very high UV index of around 9.

## A heatwave from the solstice

Aemet says temperatures will meet the criteria for a heatwave from 21 June — the summer solstice — making it the first official heatwave of the year.

## Staying safe

- Drink water regularly, especially if you are outdoors.
- Wear light, loose clothing and a hat.
- Use sun cream of SPF 30 or more and reapply every two hours.
- Plan outdoor activities for early morning or evening.
- Rest in the shade or in air-conditioned places.
- Watch for signs of heat exhaustion — dizziness, heavy sweating, nausea or headache — and move somewhere cool if you feel unwell.

People with respiratory problems should also take care while African dust is in the air, limiting strenuous exercise outdoors.
