Eivissa Daurada (Golden Ibiza) returns to Dalt Vila with a free open-air candlelight concert on the Sant Pere bastion on 18 July at 10pm.

## The setting

Hundreds of candles will light the bastion, part of the 16th-century Renaissance walls of Dalt Vila, Ibiza's UNESCO World Heritage old town. From the walls, the audience looks out over Ibiza town and the harbour — a backdrop that is very much part of the experience.

## The music

The programme combines film music, neoclassical pieces and contemporary works, with music by:

- Ludovico Einaudi;
- Hans Zimmer;
- Ennio Morricone;
- Hania Rani;
- Yann Tiersen;
- JVKE.

The selection moves from quiet, reflective piano pieces to big cinematic themes.

## The musicians

The concert is performed by musicians based in Ibiza:

| Musician | Instrument |
|---|---|
| Isaac Pérez | Violin |
| Samuel Pérez | Piano |
| Carlos Vesperinas | Cello |
| Joan Carles Marí | Percussion |
| Raquel Ortiz | Vocals |

## How to get tickets

Entry is free, but places are limited and must be booked in advance through Ibiza town council's website. Tickets tend to go quickly.

## Tips

- Arrive early to find a good spot.
- Wear comfortable shoes: Dalt Vila's streets are steep and cobbled.
- Bring a light jacket, as it can be breezy on the walls at night.

Eivissa Daurada is part of a broader effort to bring culture into Ibiza's historic spaces, giving residents and visitors a different way to experience the old town on summer nights.
