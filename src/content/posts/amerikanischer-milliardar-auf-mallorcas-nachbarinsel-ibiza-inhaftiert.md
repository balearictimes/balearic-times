James Cox Chambers, known as "Fergie", an American heir to one of the largest family fortunes in the United States, has been arrested in Ibiza at the request of the US authorities, who are seeking his extradition.

## The arrest

Chambers was detained by police on the island on Saturday. A judge ordered him to remain in custody while the extradition request is processed. The reasons for the US request have not been made public.

In Spain, extradition requests are handled by the National Court (Audiencia Nacional) in Madrid, which decides whether the legal requirements are met, with the final decision resting with the government.

## Who is he?

Chambers is a member of the family that controls Cox Enterprises, the US media and automotive services group founded by his great-grandfather, James M. Cox, an Ohio newspaper owner and 1920 presidential candidate. He has a share of the family's wealth through trusts.

He is better known, however, for his political views than for business. He describes himself as a revolutionary communist and has spoken publicly about his admiration for Marxist-Leninist leaders.

## Activism

Chambers has said he converted to Islam, and has been an outspoken supporter of the Palestinian cause since the start of the war in Gaza. He uses social media to raise funds for humanitarian projects in Gaza providing shelter and water, and has said he paid legal costs for activists linked to Palestine Action, a group that carried out direct actions against companies connected to Israel's arms industry and was later banned in the United Kingdom.

He has also been a vocal critic of US policy towards Israel and Iran, and his statements, including comments that appeared to praise the 7 October 2023 attacks on Israel, have drawn strong criticism.

## What happens next

Chambers can contest his extradition before the Spanish courts. Until the process is resolved, he will remain in custody. No details of any charges in the United States have been made public, and he has not commented publicly since his arrest.
