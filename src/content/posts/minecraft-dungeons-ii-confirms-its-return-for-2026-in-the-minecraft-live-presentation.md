Mojang's latest Minecraft Live presentation brought news on the spin-off sequel Minecraft Dungeons II and on the next free updates for the main game.

## Minecraft Dungeons II

Mojang Studios and co-developer Double Eleven confirmed that Minecraft Dungeons II is on schedule for an autumn 2026 release on:

- Xbox Series X|S and PC (Xbox app and Steam);
- PlayStation 5;
- Nintendo Switch and Switch 2.

It will be available on Game Pass from launch.

The first gameplay footage shows that the formula of the original is intact: fast dungeon-crawling action, plenty of loot and co-operative play for up to four players. The sequel promises bigger battles, new biomes, new enemies and more equipment. One of the enemies revealed is a twisted version of the Warden, the fearsome creature from the main game, which will require good teamwork to defeat.

## Chaos Cubed: the next free update

After March's Tiny Takeover update, Minecraft's next free content drop, Chaos Cubed, arrives on 16 June. It adds:

- **Sulfur caves**, a new underground biome in reds and yellows, with bubbling sulfur springs and dangerous gases that require protection;
- **new blocks**, including cinnabar and sulfur, for building and crafting;
- **the Sulfur Cube**, a new mob that absorbs blocks around it and changes its behaviour depending on what it has absorbed.

## A glimpse of autumn

Mojang also teased a further update for later in the year, showing a speckled forest with poplars, red bushes and abandoned campsites. No name has been announced, but more details are expected in the coming months.

## Still growing

More than 15 years after its release, Minecraft remains one of the best-selling and most-played games in the world, and its regular free updates are a big part of why. With a new Dungeons game on the way and the success of the Minecraft film, the franchise is in one of its busiest periods.
