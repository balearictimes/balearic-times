The heatwave that began on Sunday will intensify over the coming days, with inland Mallorca expected to reach 41–42°C on Thursday and Friday. Aemet expects the episode to ease from Thursday, but high temperatures will linger to the end of the week before a slight drop on Saturday.

## Warnings

- **Monday:** yellow warning for the south, the interior and the north and north-east of Mallorca, from midday to 7pm, with highs of around 37°C.
- **Tuesday:** yellow warning across the island except the south, with highs of around 38°C.
- **Thursday and Friday:** highs of 40°C or more across much of the island; higher warning levels are possible.

On the mainland, parts of Spain face red warnings, with temperatures of up to 44°C.

The air is very dry, with humidity as low as 15–25% inland during the afternoon.

## Sunday's readings

The highest temperatures on Sunday afternoon were 37.0°C in Llucmajor, 36.3°C at the university in Palma and 36.2°C in Binissalem.

## Staying safe

- Drink water regularly, even if you are not thirsty.
- Wear light, loose clothing and a hat.
- Avoid outdoor activity between midday and 5pm.
- Use sun cream of SPF 30 or more.
- Look out for older relatives and neighbours.
