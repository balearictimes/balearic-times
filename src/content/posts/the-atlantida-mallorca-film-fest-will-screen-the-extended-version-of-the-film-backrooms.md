The Atlàntida Mallorca Film Fest will screen a new extended version of the horror film *Backrooms* on 1 August at 10pm at La Misericòrdia in Palma, as part of the festival's Trends section.

## Sixteen new minutes

The extended cut adds 16 minutes that were not included in the cinema release. The organisers stress that this is not a collection of deleted scenes, but new material designed to expand the Backrooms universe, deepen its mysteries and give fans more clues to analyse.

## A horror hit

Despite a relatively short run in cinemas, *Backrooms* drew around 938,000 spectators in Spain, making it the most-watched horror film of the year in the country and one of the most successful genre releases of recent years.

## From YouTube to the big screen

The film is directed by Kane Parsons, a young American filmmaker who became famous in 2022 with a series of short horror videos on YouTube inspired by the "Backrooms" — an internet legend about an endless maze of empty, yellow-lit office rooms that people can supposedly fall into by accident. His videos attracted tens of millions of views and led to a feature film produced by A24.

## What to expect

The festival says the screening is a unique opportunity to see the new edit on the big screen. Fans can expect:

- new material expanding the story's lore;
- visual and sound details likely to fuel new theories;
- a reminder that there are still unexplored corners of the Backrooms world.

## The festival

The Atlàntida Mallorca Film Fest runs in Palma from 24 July to 2 August, combining screenings at venues such as La Misericòrdia with an online programme. Its Trends section focuses on films and phenomena that are shaping contemporary audiovisual culture — a fitting home for a film born on the internet.
