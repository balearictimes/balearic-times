After a hot Tuesday, Wednesday 3 June will be slightly cooler in Mallorca, with clouds inland and a small chance of a light shower, although most forecasts suggest it will stay dry.

## Wind and waves

Strong north-easterly winds will blow on Tuesday night, easing by Wednesday morning. The State Meteorological Agency (AEMET) has issued a yellow coastal alert for the north and north-east of the island from midnight to midday on Wednesday, with waves of around two metres and gusts of up to 50 km/h. Take care if you are heading to the beach or doing water sports on that coast.

Winds will generally be light to gentle, with humidity between 40% and 50%.

## Tuesday's highs

Tuesday was hot in the north and centre of the island:

- Colònia de Sant Pere: 34.1°C
- Llucmajor, Pollença and Sa Pobla: around 33.5°C
- Muro: 33.4°C
- Binissalem: 32.8°C
- Manacor: 32.7°C

The lowest minimum was 13.3°C at Son Torrella, in the Serra de Tramuntana.

## The rest of the week

- **Thursday:** warm, with highs from the mid-20s to the low 30s.
- **Friday:** a stronger north-easterly breeze and a higher chance of showers.
- **Saturday:** similar temperatures, with sun and cloud.
- **From Sunday:** temperatures are expected to rise steadily, possibly leading to a warm spell lasting several days.

## Sea safety

North-easterly winds can make beaches on the north coast — such as those in the bays of Pollença and Alcúdia — dangerous, with waves and currents even on sunny days. Always check the flag, follow lifeguards' instructions and avoid swimming in rough sea.
