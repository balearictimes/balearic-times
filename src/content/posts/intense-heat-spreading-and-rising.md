Mallorca is in the grip of intense heat that is set to spread and strengthen over the coming days. Temperatures reached 36°C in the north and north-east of the island and 37°C inland and in the south, and forecasters expect them to climb further.

## The hottest places

| Location | Maximum |
|---|---|
| Binissalem | 37.6°C |
| Muro | 37.4°C |
| Santa Maria del Camí | 37.3°C |
| Llucmajor | 37.0°C |
| Palma | 36.9°C |

## The forecast

- **North, north-east and inland Mallorca:** up to 38°C.
- **Serra de Tramuntana and the east of the island:** around 37°C.
- **Ibiza and Formentera:** highs close to 36°C.

The heat is expected to last through midweek, with a yellow warning for high temperatures in force across the Balearic Islands on Wednesday.

## What a yellow warning means

A yellow warning indicates that temperatures are high enough to pose a risk to vulnerable people and those working or exercising outdoors, although they are not exceptional for the time of year.

## Staying safe

The Balearic emergency service, 112, advises taking particular care between midday and 4pm:

- drink plenty of water, even if you are not thirsty;
- stay in the shade or in cool places;
- wear light, loose clothing and a hat;
- apply sun cream regularly;
- avoid strenuous exercise during the hottest hours;
- never leave children, older people or pets in a parked car, even for a few minutes.

Signs of heat exhaustion include headache, dizziness, nausea, heavy sweating and cramps. Anyone affected should move to a cool place, drink water and rest. If symptoms worsen or the person becomes confused, call 112.
