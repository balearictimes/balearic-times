## Introduction to Halloween Celebrations
The Ajuntament de Sant Antoni de Portmany has prepared a programme of activities to celebrate Halloween and Tots Sants, with proposals for all ages and distributed across different spaces in the municipality. The goal is to offer leisure alternatives and encourage participation during these dates.

## Activities for All Ages
The Espai Jove has organised various activities to enjoy terrifyingly fun days. On 30 October, a tunnel of terror will take place, with passes for children aged 6-11 from 17:30 to 18:30 and for young people aged 12-16 from 19:00 to 20:00. On 31 October, a costume party will be held from 9:30 to 13:30, exclusively for users registered for the activity. Dracs d’Eivissa will offer board games, gymkhanas, and traditional 'trick or treat' activities from 17:00 to 21:00. To end the day, a special Halloween dinner will be held for members from 21:00 to 00:00, with a price of 15 euros and three exclusive raffles.

## Additional Activities and Events
On 1 November, the Espai Jove will host workshops and Halloween-themed activities for all audiences from 12:00 to 20:30, courtesy of the Associació Otaku d’Eivissa. The Sant Rafel festivities will also take place on 1 November with the Festa infantil de Tots Sants, featuring traditional fruit breaking, face painting, inflatable castles, a raffle, and various family activities from 10:00 to 13:00. The Municipal Library will participate in the programme with a special Halloween book table from 26 to 30 October, featuring terrifying stories and adventures for readers of all ages. A handicraft workshop will be held on 30 October from 17:30 to 19:00, aimed at children aged 6-9, and requires prior registration via email.

## Collaboration with Educational Centres
The Ajuntament is also collaborating with the Halloween festivities of various educational centres in the municipality. The CEIP Sant Antoni already celebrated its event on 23 October, while the CEIP Guillem de Montgrí will organise a party on 30 October and the CC Can Bonet on 31 October. These events will feature workshops, inflatable castles, tunnels of terror, and plenty of fun for children and young people.
