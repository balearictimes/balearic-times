After a hot start to the week, Mallorca could see some light rain on Wednesday and Thursday, particularly in the centre and east of the island. Showers are expected to be light and scattered, with only a small chance of an isolated thunderstorm.

## Wednesday and Thursday

- A reasonable chance of showers in central and eastern areas.
- Any rain is likely to be light and patchy.
- On Thursday, a yellow warning for coastal conditions is in force on the north and north-east coast.

## Temperatures

Temperatures will dip slightly over the next couple of days before rising again early next week, but most of the island will still be above 30°C. The UV index is extreme, at 10.

## Tuesday's readings

The highest temperature on Tuesday afternoon was 36.6°C in Llucmajor, with 34°C at Palma airport. The coolest overnight reading was 14.5°C at Son Torrella in the Tramuntana.

## Friday

A fairly strong north-easterly wind is forecast for Friday, making the coast feel fresher. Good news for windsurfers and sailors; less so for anyone hoping for a calm beach day. Secure parasols and towels on the sand.

## Tips

- Carry a light waterproof on Wednesday and Thursday, especially if heading to the north-east coast.
- If you are hiking in the Tramuntana, start early and check the forecast before setting off.
- With UV at extreme levels, use high-factor sun cream and reapply after swimming.
