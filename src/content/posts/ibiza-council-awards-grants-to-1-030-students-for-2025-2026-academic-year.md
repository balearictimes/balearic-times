## Ibiza Council Grants Announced
The Consell Insular d'Eivissa has completed the processing of the Line 1 grants for students for the 2025-2026 academic year. A total of 1,030 students from the island will receive grants, with a total value of 999,996.81 euros, entirely funded by the Consell's own funds.

## Breakdown of Grants
Of these grants, 905 will go to students studying for university degrees, higher artistic education, or vocational training outside of Ibiza, with each student receiving 1,020.77 euros, totaling 923,796.85 euros. Additionally, 62 students studying for master's degrees outside the island will receive grants of 822.58 euros, totaling 50,999.96 euros, and 63 students studying for university degrees or master's degrees on the island will receive grants of 400 euros, totaling 25,200 euros.

## Increased Funding
The 2026 call for grants has seen an increase of almost 43% in funding, from 700,000 euros in the previous year to over 1 million euros. This increase has allowed the number of beneficiaries to exceed 1,000 for the first time. The budget forecast for 2027 maintains a funding allocation of over 1 million euros to continue these grants.

## Next Steps
The resolution also allows for the payment to be made earlier than in previous years, with the aim of providing students and their families with financial support sooner to help offset the costs of studying on or off the island. Regarding Line 2, which corresponds to the travel grants from the Balearic Government for university studies outside of Ibiza, a provisional proposal has been published, including 470 grants of 757.63 euros, totaling 356,086.10 euros. The deadline for submitting allegations to this proposal is from November 20 to December 3. The resolutions and lists can be consulted on the Consell Insular d'Eivissa's Electronic Headquarters and notice board.
