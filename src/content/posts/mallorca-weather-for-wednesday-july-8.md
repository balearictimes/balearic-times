Aemet says its heatwave episode will end on Thursday, but Mallorca will remain very hot for several more days, with inland highs of up to 41°C expected on Thursday and Friday. Artà reached 40.2°C on Tuesday.

## Warnings

**Wednesday**
- Amber warnings for the north and north-east, the interior and the east, from midday to 8pm, with highs of 39–40°C.
- Yellow warnings for the Tramuntana and the south, with highs of around 37°C.

**Thursday**
- Amber warnings across the island except the Tramuntana.

Yellow warnings may continue into the weekend.

## Very dry air

Humidity will be very low during the day, as little as 15% inland. The UV index will be high, at 8.

## Tuesday's hottest places

Artà (40.2°C), Petra (39.1°C), Sa Pobla (38.2°C), Llucmajor and Pollença (38.1°C), and Sineu (37.6°C) were among the hottest places on Tuesday. Even Lluc, in the mountains, reached 36.8°C. The coolest overnight reading was 13.3°C at Son Torrella.

## Staying safe

- Drink water regularly throughout the day.
- Wear a hat, sunglasses and light, loose clothing.
- Use sun cream of SPF 30 or more and reapply it every two hours.
- Avoid the sun and physical effort during the warning hours, from midday to 8pm.
- Watch for signs of heat exhaustion — heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, nausea — and move somewhere cool if you feel unwell.

With very dry air and high temperatures, the risk of wildfires is also high: avoid any activity that could cause a spark in the countryside.
