Mallorca has had only a small break from the extreme heat. Saturday's maximum dropped from 40°C on Friday to 39°C, and Sunday will be a little cloudier and breezier — but a fresh mass of warm air from Africa means temperatures will not fall much, and they are expected to climb again next week.

## Warnings

Yellow heat warnings are in force for almost all of Mallorca, except the eastern tip, from midday to 8pm on Sunday, with highs of around 38°C. The warnings continue on Monday.

## The week ahead

Temperatures are forecast to rise again from Monday, with many towns reaching the mid to high 30s and some inland areas passing 40°C by midweek. If the forecast holds, Thursday and Friday could bring highs of up to 43°C inland, which would be among the highest temperatures recorded on the island.

Winds will be mainly from the east, moderate at times, especially in the south-east.

## What extreme heat means

- A higher risk of heat-related illness, particularly for older people, young children and anyone exercising outdoors.
- Higher demand for electricity and water.
- The possibility of isolated afternoon storms where hot air meets sea breezes.

## Staying safe

- Drink water regularly throughout the day.
- Wear light, loose clothing and a hat.
- Plan outdoor activities for early morning or evening.
- Use sun cream of SPF 30 or more and reapply every two hours.
- Rest in the shade or in air-conditioned places.
- Check on vulnerable relatives and neighbours.
