Motorworld Mallorca, near Palma, hosts an evening of "Music & Talk" on Wednesday 24 June, combining live music from songwriter Willi Meyer and his band with two well-known guests from German television.

## Willi Meyer and his band

Meyer returns to the stage with a full band:

- Soriana Ivani – violin and vocals
- Patti Balinas – drums and vocals
- Silke Hamann – vocals
- Brahm Heidl – bass
- LotR – guitar
- Daniel Nadal Molina – keyboards
- Willi Meyer – guitar and vocals

## Mola Adebisi

The first guest is Mola Adebisi, who became one of the faces of German music television in the 1990s as a presenter on the music channel VIVA. He went on to release the single "Shake That Body", act in series including *Marienhof*, and appear twice on RTL's jungle reality show. More recently he has toured as a DJ. The audience will find out whether he simply shares stories from his career or joins the band on stage.

## Volker Wolf

The second guest is Volker Wolf, a voice actor known to German audiences as the voice of Jean-Claude Van Damme in many films and as a narrator on ZDF's popular programme *Bares für Rares*. He has also recorded audiobooks and voiced cartoon characters. At Motorworld he will step out of the recording booth to give a live reading.

## What to expect

- Willi Meyer's songs performed with a full band.
- Conversation, anecdotes and stories from the guests.
- A relaxed evening mixing music, storytelling and nostalgia.

## Practical information

- **Date:** Wednesday 24 June
- **Doors:** 8pm
- **Venue:** Motorworld Mallorca, near Palma
- **Tickets:** €30 in advance, €35 on the door
- **Dinner:** tables can be booked at the venue's restaurant

## About the venue

Motorworld Mallorca combines a showroom of classic and luxury cars with restaurants and event spaces, and has become a regular venue for events aimed at the island's large German-speaking community. Evenings like this one reflect the strong cultural ties between Mallorca and Germany, where many residents and visitors grew up with the guests on their television screens.
