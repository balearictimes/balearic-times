Guerrilla Games, the studio behind *Horizon Zero Dawn* and *Horizon Forbidden West*, has announced *Horizon Hunters Gathering*, an online co-operative game set in the Horizon universe, for PlayStation 5 and PC.

## From idea to full game

Guerrilla says the concept of hunting machines together dates back to early brainstorming for the series and has gone through several prototypes before becoming a full project.

## Hunting as a team

Squads of up to three players work together to take down the robotic creatures that roam the world of Horizon. The studio promises tactical, skill-based combat that builds on the precise mechanics of the main games — targeting weak points, using the right weapons and elements — but with a much greater emphasis on teamwork.

## Two modes

- **Machine incursion:** teams fight waves of increasingly dangerous machines emerging from underground strongholds.
- **Descent into the Cauldron:** longer missions with high-risk encounters, unique rewards and hidden story secrets.

## Hunters and a social hub

Players choose from a range of hunters, each with their own abilities, personality and story, which develop through the campaign.

Between missions, players return to the Hunters Gathering, a social hub where they can:

- customise their characters and equipment;
- meet other players and non-player characters;
- form new squads, with cross-play between PS5 and PC.

Progress will carry over between the two platforms.

## How to get in early

A closed beta starts in late February 2026. Players can register through the PlayStation Beta Program using their PlayStation Network account. No release date has been announced.

## The wider Horizon universe

Guerrilla is also working with the Korean company NCSOFT on *Horizon Steel Frontiers*, a massively multiplayer online game for PC and mobile. Together, the two projects show Sony expanding one of its most successful series beyond single-player adventures — following a broader industry trend towards games designed to be played with friends over long periods.
