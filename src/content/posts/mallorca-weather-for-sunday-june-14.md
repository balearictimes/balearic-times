Humidity will be low inland, around 25–30%, and higher on the coast.

## Saturday's highs

The highest temperatures on Saturday were recorded in the south and centre of the island:

- Porreres: 33.3°C
- Llucmajor: 33.1°C
- Sant Elm: 32.4°C
- Es Capdellà: 32.3°C
- Cap Blanc: 32.1°C
- Palma (university): 31.7°C
- Binissalem: 31.6°C
- Manacor: 31.1°C

## Cool nights inland

Despite the heat of the day, nights are still fresh in some areas. The lowest minimums were 10.8°C at Can Sion (Campos), 11.8°C at Lluc and 12.2°C at the university in Palma.

## The week ahead

Temperatures will gradually rise over the coming days. From Wednesday, the interior of the island could reach around 36°C, close to the threshold for a yellow heat warning. Coastal areas will remain a few degrees cooler thanks to sea breezes.

## Staying safe in the sun

With a UV index of 8, unprotected skin can burn within minutes. Remember to:

- use sunscreen of at least factor 30 and reapply it regularly, especially after swimming;
- wear a hat, sunglasses and light clothing;
- drink water throughout the day;
- avoid strenuous activity, such as hiking or cycling, in the middle of the day;
- seek shade between noon and 5pm.
