## Introduction to Inclusive Sports
The Consell d'Eivissa's Department of Sports has organised the sixth edition of the DIE Inclusive Sports at School programme, which aims to promote inclusive sports among students. The programme, co-organised with the Càtedra Fundación Sanitas d’Estudis sobre Esport Inclusiu (CEDI) and the Ibiza Town Council's departments of Culture and Sports, has brought together 250 students from the Col·legi Nostra Senyora de la Consolació and world champion athlete Javier Soto Rey.

## Practical Sessions and Workshops
The programme includes practical sessions and workshops where students can participate in various adaptive sports, such as seated volleyball, goalball, and athletics. The sessions are designed to raise awareness about the barriers that people with disabilities may face and to promote inclusion and participation. Javier Soto, who is deaf, shared his experiences and answered questions from the students, and also led an athletics session with the help of a sign language interpreter.

## Expansion to Other Islands
The programme has been successful in Ibiza, with nearly 8,000 student participations over the past five editions. The Consell d'Eivissa's initiative has also attracted the attention of the Balearic Government's Service for Inclusion in the Educational Community (SICE), which has sent representatives to Ibiza to learn about the programme and consider its implementation in schools on Mallorca, Menorca, and Formentera.

## Future Plans
The sixth edition of the programme, which started on October 15, will continue throughout the school year with sessions at other educational centres on the island. The programme's organisers aim to promote values such as constancy, teamwork, and companionship, and to inspire students to participate in inclusive sports. As Javier Soto said, through his participation, the programme aims to work with students on values such as "constància" and "companyerisme". The Consell d'Eivissa's Department of Sports will continue to develop and expand the programme, with the goal of promoting inclusive sports and physical activity among students on the island.
