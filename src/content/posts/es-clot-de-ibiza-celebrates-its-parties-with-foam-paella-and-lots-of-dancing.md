The Es Clot neighbourhood in Ibiza town is holding its summer festes from Sunday 19 to Sunday 26 July, with a programme of games, music, dance and food for all ages.

## Sunday 19 July

- **10am:** first Es Clot open chess tournament.

## Friday 24 July

- **6.30pm:** foam party.
- **8pm:** capoeira display.
- **9pm:** concert by Ibiza's municipal symphonic band.
- **10.30pm:** dance show.

## Saturday 25 July

- **Midday:** foam party.
- **7pm:** children's party with face painting.
- **7pm:** live music and a dance session for older residents.
- **9pm:** neighbourhood supper, with residents sharing dishes, and a DJ set.
- **10.30pm:** live concert.

## Sunday 26 July

- **11am:** farewell foam party.
- **2pm:** popular paella for everyone.
- **8pm:** dance music.
- **9.30pm:** concert.
- **Midnight:** end of the festes.

## Photography exhibition

During August, a photography exhibition featuring work by photographers from Es Clot will be open to the public.

## Neighbourhood festes

Neighbourhood festes are an important part of summer in Ibiza town. Beyond the island's international nightlife, these local celebrations are organised by residents' associations, often with the support of the town council, and are a chance for neighbours of all ages to come together. Foam parties are a favourite with children in the summer heat, while the popular paella and the shared supper are classic moments of community life.

For visitors, events like these offer a different view of Ibiza: a local celebration in which families, older residents and young people share the street, the music and the food.
