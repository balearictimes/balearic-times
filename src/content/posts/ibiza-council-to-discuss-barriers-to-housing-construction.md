## Introduction to the Conference
The Consell d'Eivissa, Ibiza's island council, will host a conference this Wednesday, 18 November, to discuss the barriers that hinder the construction of housing on the island. The event, titled "Més habitatge, menys barreres: com avançam?" (More housing, fewer barriers: how do we move forward?), will take place at the Sala de Plens from 17:00 to 20:00.

## Conference Programme
The conference will feature three presentations by experts in housing, economics, and real estate finance. Ignacio José de Andrés, councillor for Environmental Management, will introduce the conference. Héctor Simón Moreno, director of the UNESCO Chair of Housing at the Universitat Rovira i Virgili, will discuss the role of regulations and public policies in the development of the housing market. Konstantin Kholodilin, senior researcher at the German Institute for Economic Research (DIW Berlin), will analyse international experiences and the effects of regulatory barriers on housing supply. Omar E. García Glumaz, director of the Real Estate Business Centre at CaixaBank, will address the financial obstacles to housing production.

## Round Table Discussion
After the presentations, a round table discussion will be held, moderated by Sofia Ribas, founder of (Ma), Laboratori de Transformació Sostenible. The discussion will feature the three speakers and will provide an opportunity for attendees to share their perspectives on the barriers to housing construction and possible solutions.

## Context and Objective
The conference is part of the "Eivissa: habitatge, territori i futur" (Ibiza: housing, territory, and future) cycle, which aims to create a space for reflection and debate on the island's housing challenges. The cycle, initiated by the Consell d'Eivissa in February, seeks to consolidate a plural dialogue space to discuss diagnoses, proposals, and experiences related to the population's housing needs and the decisions that will shape the island's future. The event is open to the public, with free entry, and will take place at the Sala de Plens, located at Avinguda d'Espanya, 49. According to the Consell d'Eivissa, the objective is to "contrastar diferents perspectives" (contrast different perspectives) on the housing challenges facing the island.
