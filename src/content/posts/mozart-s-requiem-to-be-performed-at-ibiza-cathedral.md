## Introduction to the Event
Ibiza Cathedral will host a special concert on November 1, featuring Mozart's Requiem, to commemorate the 270th anniversary of the composer's birth. The event, which coincides with All Saints' Day, will bring together soloists, choir, and orchestra in one of the island's most emblematic heritage spaces.

## The Concert Programme
The programme will include two compositions by Mozart: the Laudate Dominum, KV 339, and the Requiem in D minor, KV 626, which was left unfinished at the time of the composer's death in 1791. According to the Consellera de Cultura, Educació i Patrimoni, Sara Ramon, "La música de Mozart forma part del patrimoni cultural universal" and this concert is an opportunity to enjoy classical music while rediscovering the value of the island's historic spaces.

## Performance Details
The concert will feature four soloists: Tanya Durán-Gil, soprano; Adriana Mayer, mezzosoprano; Néster Martorell, tenor; and Sebastià Peris, bass. The Cor de Cambra Amalthea, directed by Marta Mármol, and the Jove Orquestra Illes Balears, linked to Joan Barceló, will also participate. The musical direction of the concert will be led by Juan F. Ballesteros.

## Practical Information
The concert, which is free to attend, will take place on November 1 at 12:00 pm at Ibiza Cathedral. The event is a collaboration between the Consell, the Bishopric of Ibiza, and various musical entities and businesses on the island. More information about the programme and performers can be found on the website www.just4you.es/requiem-ibiza/. The Consell d'Eivissa, the island council, has highlighted the cultural value of this musical proposal and the uniqueness of the chosen venue for its interpretation.
