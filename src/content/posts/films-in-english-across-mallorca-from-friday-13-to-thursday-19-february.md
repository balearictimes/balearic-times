Here is our guide to the films showing in original version in Mallorca's cinemas this week. Showtimes change frequently, so check with each cinema before going.

## New releases

**Wuthering Heights (2026).** Emerald Fennell, director of *Promising Young Woman* and *Saltburn*, brings her own intense, provocative vision to Emily Brontë's novel, with Margot Robbie as Catherine and Jacob Elordi as Heathcliff. Expect passion, revenge and heartbreak on the Yorkshire moors.

*Where:* Augusta Aficine and CineCiutat in Palma, Ocine Premium at Porto Pi, Cinesa Festival Park in Marratxí and Artesiete Fan.

**GOAT (2026).** An animated family comedy about a small goat who dreams of making it in "roarball", a fast, high-contact sport dominated by much bigger animals. With the voices of Caleb McLaughlin, Gabrielle Union and Nick Kroll.

*Where:* Ocine Premium, Porto Pi.

## Valentine's week classics

- **(500) Days of Summer (2009).** Joseph Gordon-Levitt and Zooey Deschanel in a romantic comedy that warns from the start that it is not a love story. *Cinesa Festival Park.*
- **10 Things I Hate About You (1999).** Heath Ledger and Julia Stiles in a high-school update of Shakespeare's *The Taming of the Shrew*. *Cinesa Festival Park.*
- **Romeo + Juliet (1996).** Baz Luhrmann's explosive version of Shakespeare, with Leonardo DiCaprio and Claire Danes. *Cinesa Festival Park.*

## Still showing

- **Marty Supreme (2025).** Timothée Chalamet stars in Josh Safdie's drama about an ambitious table tennis hustler in 1950s New York. *Augusta Aficine, Cinesa Festival Park, CineCiutat.*
- **Hamnet (2025).** Chloé Zhao's adaptation of Maggie O'Farrell's novel, with Jessie Buckley and Paul Mescal as Shakespeare's wife and husband grieving the loss of their son. *Ocine Premium, Rivoli Aficine, CineCiutat.*
- **Avatar: Fire and Ash (2025).** James Cameron's latest return to Pandora, in 3D. *Ocine Premium.*
- **The Chronology of Water (2025).** Kristen Stewart's directorial debut, with Imogen Poots as a swimmer who finds her voice through writing. *CineCiutat.*

## Where to watch films in original version

Palma's CineCiutat specialises in original-version cinema, while the larger multiplexes at Porto Pi, Festival Park and Fan Mallorca regularly include English-language sessions. Many sessions are marked "VOSE" — original version with Spanish subtitles.
