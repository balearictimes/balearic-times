## Introduction to Emergency Preparedness
The town council of Sant Antoni de Portmany has taken steps to enhance the preparedness and coordination of its municipal services in the event of emergencies. This week, the council implemented the Municipal Emergency Plan (PEMU) and the Municipal Flood Risk Action Plan (PAMIN), with the goal of improving the response to potential emergency situations.

## Implementation of Emergency Plans
The implementation of these emergency plans is a mandatory process following their approval, allowing the theoretical planning to be put into practice and ensuring that the services involved are aware of their functions, procedures, and the main risks existing in the municipality. The activities carried out this week aimed to improve the operationality of the plans, facilitate direct knowledge of critical points in the territory, and strengthen the response capacity of the municipal teams.

## Training and Field Work
The programme consisted of two parts: theoretical training on organisation and procedures, and a practical fieldwork day to identify the main risk scenarios and strategic infrastructure included in the plans. The theoretical training sessions, led by Geolat, the consultancy responsible for developing both plans, covered the municipal organisation in emergency situations, the functioning of the Municipal Operational Coordination Centre (CECOPAL), and the responsibilities of the different action groups. The main risks in the municipality, with special attention to flooding, were also analysed to improve knowledge of the scenarios that may require coordinated intervention from municipal services.

## Next Steps
A fieldwork day allowed participants to visit critical points in the municipality, including flood-prone areas in Passeig de la Mar and the beach of s'Arenal, as well as intersections of torrents with roads. The itinerary also included the Montecristo industrial estate, Cala Salada, the CECOPAL, and the Can Coix sports complex. During the visits, the existing risks, potential effects in the event of an emergency, and preventive and response measures were analysed. The next step in the implementation process will be a simulation exercise on 18 November, followed by an information session for the public at the town council's plenary hall. With these actions, the Sant Antoni de Portmany town council continues to advance in the preparation of its municipal services and the improvement of prevention and coordination mechanisms, with the objective of having operative tools to act effectively in the event of emergencies and guarantee the safety of citizens.
