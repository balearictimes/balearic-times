Wednesday 17 June will be mostly sunny and hot in Mallorca, with temperatures of 30°C or more across most of the island and light winds. There are no heat warnings for now, but forecasters expect them in the coming days, especially inland.

## Next week

Weather models suggest that inland Mallorca could reach **40°C** on Monday. In the interior of the island, a yellow heat warning is issued from 36°C and an amber warning from 39–40°C, so the island is already close to the thresholds.

Humidity will be very low inland, around 20%. The chance of rain is practically zero.

## Tuesday's figures

The highest temperature on Tuesday afternoon was 35.3°C in Porreres. Nights are still relatively fresh in the mountains: the lowest minimum was 9.4°C at Son Torrella, in Escorca.

## Preparing for the heat

With temperatures set to climb further, it is worth getting ready:

- drink water regularly, even if you're not thirsty;
- wear light, loose clothing and a hat outdoors;
- plan outdoor activities and exercise for early morning or evening;
- use sunscreen of at least factor 30 and reapply it regularly;
- keep blinds down during the day and ventilate at night;
- check on older relatives and neighbours, who are most vulnerable.

If you feel dizzy, nauseous or unusually tired, move to a cool place and rest. Look out for official warnings from the State Meteorological Agency (AEMET), which may be issued for the start of next week.
