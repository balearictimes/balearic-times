## Introduction to the Autumn Programme
The Consell d'Eivissa's Department of Culture, Education and Heritage has announced its autumn programme at the Cas Serres Auditorium, featuring four shows that combine theatre, humour, and dance. The programme, which runs from November to December, includes three productions that showcase local talent and humour, as well as a special performance to commemorate International Flamenco Day.

## The Shows
The first show, Baleares Comedy Club, will take place on November 14th at 8pm, as part of the 17th edition of the Festival de l'Humor d'Eivissa. The show will feature comedians Dani Morla, Jaime Gili, and José Boto, who will explore the differences and quirks of each island in the Balearics through humour. The following day, November 15th, the auditorium will host La prodigiosa Zapatera, a dance performance by the Ibérica de Danza company, which reinterprets Federico García Lorca's tragicomedy through a combination of dance, music, and contemporary stage techniques.

In December, the programme will continue with Ses honorables virtuts il·lògiques, a multidisciplinary show featuring poet and performer Jèssica Ferrer Escandell and clown and musician Diego Martínez González. The show will combine music, word, movement, humour, and objects to create a unique scenic experience. Finally, on December 13th, the auditorium will host the comedy Ocho apellidos Ibicencos, written and performed by José Boto and a cast of eight local actors.

## Ticket Information
Tickets for the four shows can be purchased in advance through the website www.ticketib.com for 8 euros, or directly at the Cas Serres Auditorium box office for 12 euros. According to the consellera de Cultura, Educació i Patrimoni, Sara Ramon, "this programme combines different artistic disciplines and gives special presence to creators from our island, while also allowing the public to enjoy productions from companies with a consolidated trajectory". The objective, she added, is to "continue offering a varied and accessible cultural programme, with proposals for different audiences and ages".
