## Introduction to the New Awards
The Consell d'Eivissa, the island council of Ibiza, has initially approved a new regulation for the Ibiza Sports Awards, updating the existing rules that have been in place since 2008. The new regulation incorporates eight new awards and gives more visibility to school sports, inclusion, and the work of individuals and entities that make sports activities possible on the island.

## New Categories and Participation
One of the main novelties is the inclusion of awards for the best young athlete in school age, in both female and male categories. These distinctions will allow for the recognition of the merits of young athletes and give more prominence to grassroots sports. The other six new awards will recognize the best sports event, referee, socio-sports project, volunteer, coach, and sports dissemination. According to the Consell, the new regulation also expands the participation of sports entities in the nominations and the jury.

## Changes to the Existing Awards
The review redefines the award for the "best individual" to "best sports career", to express more clearly the purpose of this distinction. The Consell said the new regulation maintains recognitions for the best federation delegation, sports team, sports club, sponsoring entity, and athlete. The conseller de Presidència, Hisenda, Gestió Econòmica i Esports, Salvador Losa, highlighted the incorporation of explicit recognition of the promotion of inclusion in sports.

## Next Steps
The proposal incorporates the majority of the contributions received during the public consultation and from the different political groups. After the initial approval, the regulation will be subject to a 30-day public consultation period, during which allegations, complaints, and suggestions can be presented before the final approval. The Consell will then consider these contributions before making a final decision on the new regulation.
