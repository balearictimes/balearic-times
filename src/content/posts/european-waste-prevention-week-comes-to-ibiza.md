## Introduction to European Waste Prevention Week
The Consell d'Eivissa is participating in the European Waste Prevention Week, which will take place from November 21 to 29, 2026. The event aims to raise awareness about the importance of reducing waste, promoting reuse, and adopting more sustainable consumption habits. This year's initiative focuses on material sufficiency, encouraging people to reflect on their consumption needs and use resources more efficiently.

## Activities and Events
The Consell d'Eivissa has prepared a range of activities in collaboration with the Zero Food Waste project, promoted by Herbusa and the UTE Giref. The program includes informational stands, a visit to the Ca na Putxa waste treatment facilities, and a cooking workshop on food waste reduction. On November 21, an informational stand on waste reduction and recycling will be set up at the Mercat Nou d'Eivissa, while on November 22, a similar stand will be available at the Mercat Artesanal de Sant Joan de Labritja, focusing on food waste reduction.

## Practical Details
The visit to Ca na Putxa on November 26 is open to the public, but prior registration is required due to limited spaces. The bus will depart from the Consell Insular d'Eivissa building at 9:30 am and return at 2:00 pm. On November 27, a practical cooking workshop will be held at the Escola d'Hoteleria de les Illes Balears in Sa Coma, where participants will learn to create recipes that make the most of food and reduce waste. Registration for the visit and the workshop can be done by emailing info@residuseivissa.com or calling 900 26 48 78.

## Getting Involved
For more information about the activities and the European Waste Prevention Week, residents can visit the website residus.conselldeivissa.es or follow the social media profiles @residuseivissa and @residus_eivissa. The Consell d'Eivissa encourages everyone to participate in the events and take steps towards reducing waste and promoting sustainability on the island. As the initiative's slogan says, "Qüestionam les nostres decisions, valoram els nostres recursos!" ("Let's question our decisions, let's value our resources!").
