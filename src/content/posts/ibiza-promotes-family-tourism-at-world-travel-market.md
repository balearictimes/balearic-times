## Introduction to Family Tourism in Ibiza
The Consell Insular d'Eivissa (Ibiza Island Council) has presented the main conclusions of a study on family tourism in Ibiza at the World Travel Market in London. The study confirms the strategic importance of this segment and identifies areas for improvement to strengthen its position.

## Key Findings of the Study
The study, which analyzed over 4,000 surveys of visitors traveling with families, found that family tourism accounts for approximately 25% of visitors to the island. These visitors are characterized by their loyalty, with 58% of them returning to the island, and a high level of satisfaction, with an average rating of 87 out of 100. The study also found that 57% of family visitors stay on the island for seven days or more, resulting in a high value per stay. According to the Consell, "la fidelitat de les famílies" (family loyalty) is a key asset for the destination.

## Opportunities and Challenges
The study identifies opportunities for growth in the family tourism sector, including the spring and autumn seasons, which could attract visitors from European markets. However, it also highlights challenges such as the high cost of accommodation, perceived overcrowding during peak season, and the island's image as a destination for nightlife. To address these challenges, the study proposes developing an offer specifically designed for families, with experiences adapted to different ages and family types.

## Future Plans
The Consell plans to use the study's conclusions to guide the evolution of Ibiza Family Moments, a initiative that aims to promote family tourism on the island. The Consell also plans to create a public-private working group to share knowledge, establish priorities, and implement actions to strengthen the family tourism sector. The goal is to develop a roadmap by 2030 to improve the experience of families, enhance the competitiveness of the offer, and consolidate this segment within the diversity of Ibiza's tourism industry.
