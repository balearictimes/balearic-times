Aldi is bringing a little of Formentera to the dinner table this summer with a new tableware collection inspired by the smallest of the Balearic Islands.

The range goes on sale in its Spanish stores from Saturday 4 July, in limited quantities.

## The dinner set

The centrepiece is a 12-piece stoneware set in turquoise glazes, with irregular, wave-like edges meant to evoke the sea around the island. It contains:

- four dinner plates (26 cm);
- four soup plates (21 cm);
- four dessert plates (20 cm).

The full set costs €29.99, which Aldi says is 43% below its usual price. The plates are also sold in pairs: dinner or soup plates at €6.99 a pair, and dessert plates at €4.99 a pair, so shoppers can buy only what they need or replace breakages.

## Glasses and accessories

To go with the plates, the collection includes a pack of six coloured glass tumblers in blues and greens, each holding 47.5 cl, for €7.99. There is also a selection of natural-fibre items — woven placemats, baskets and small table accessories — at €2.50 each.

## Why stoneware

Stoneware is a ceramic fired at very high temperatures, typically 1,200–1,300°C. That makes it dense and less porous than ordinary earthenware, so it resists chipping, staining and sudden changes in temperature better. It has a slightly rustic finish compared with porcelain and is usually safe in the dishwasher and microwave, which makes it practical for everyday use and for eating outdoors.

## Availability

As with all of Aldi's weekly non-food offers, the Formentera range is available only while stocks last and the company does not guarantee a restock. The full sets, in particular, tend to sell out quickly.
