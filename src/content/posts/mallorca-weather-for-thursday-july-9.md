Thursday will be one of the hottest days of the summer so far in Mallorca, with an amber heat warning for most of the island and highs of up to 41°C.

## Warnings

- **Thursday:** amber warning for most of the island from midday to 8pm, with highs of around 41°C; yellow warning in the Serra de Tramuntana, with highs of around 38°C.
- **Friday:** amber warning across the island except the east, which remains on yellow.
- **Weekend:** yellow warnings are likely on Saturday and Sunday.

Temperatures will ease slightly at the weekend, but inland areas could still reach 41°C again early next week.

The air will be very dry inland, with humidity as low as 10–15% in the afternoon, and winds generally light.

## Tips to stay cool

- **Drink water regularly,** even if you are not thirsty.
- **Stay in the shade** or indoors during the hottest hours, from midday to the evening.
- **Wear light clothing** — loose, light-coloured fabrics help sweat evaporate.
- **Keep your home cool:** close blinds during the day and ventilate at night.
- **Look after others:** older neighbours, young children and pets are particularly vulnerable.

## When to seek help

Heatstroke is a medical emergency. Signs include a very high body temperature, hot dry skin, confusion and loss of consciousness. Call 112 immediately and cool the person down while you wait for help.
