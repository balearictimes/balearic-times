## Introduction to La Prodigiosa Zapatera
The Consell d'Eivissa is commemorating International Flamenco Day, observed on November 16, with a special event. This Sunday, November 15, at 7 pm, the Auditori del Centre Sociocultural de Cas Serres will host La Prodigiosa Zapatera, a production by the renowned Ibérica de Danza company.

## The Performance
La Prodigiosa Zapatera is an original adaptation of Federico García Lorca's tragicomedy La zapatera prodigiosa. The show combines the expressive power of Spanish dance and flamenco with innovative stage design. Under the artistic direction of Manuel Segovia, the performance explores the female universe, individual freedom, and human emotions, maintaining the essence of Lorca's work from a contemporary perspective.

## Details and Tickets
The event promises to be a unique experience, with rich choreographies, skilled dancers, and a soundtrack that combines original compositions, classical themes, and melodies inspired by García Lorca's musical universe. The use of audiovisual projection techniques and mapping adds to the poetic and theatrical dimension of the performance. Tickets are available in advance for 8 euros until two hours before the show and for 12 euros at the box office until ten minutes before the start. They can be purchased through the platform www.ticketib.com.

## Information and Contact
For more information, interested individuals can contact the Consell d'Eivissa's cultural programming service via email at programacio.cultural@conselldeivissa.es or by phone at 680 104 856, from Monday to Friday, 10 am to 2 pm. The Consell d'Eivissa, or Ibiza Island Council, is the governing body of the island, responsible for promoting and preserving its culture and heritage. This event is part of its efforts to celebrate and promote flamenco, a significant part of Spanish cultural identity.
