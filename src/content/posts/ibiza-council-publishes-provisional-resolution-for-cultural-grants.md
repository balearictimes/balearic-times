## Introduction to the Provisional Resolution
The Consell d'Eivissa, the island council of Ibiza, has published the provisional resolution for economic grants to support cultural activities related to publishing, cultural promotion, and municipal music schools for the year 2026. According to the Consell d'Eivissa, the provisional resolution corresponds to applications submitted under modalities A and B of the call.

## Details of the Provisional Resolution
Applicants can consult the provisional resolution and submit allegations until November 13, 2026. The allegations must be submitted through a general instance on the Consell Insular d'Eivissa's Electronic Headquarters (https://seu.conselldeivissa.es), indicating the assigned case number. Individuals can also submit allegations in person at the enabled registries. The Consell d'Eivissa said that the deadline for submitting allegations is ten working days from the day after the publication.

## Additional Information for Students
The Department of Culture, Education, and Heritage of the Consell Insular d'Eivissa has also published a list of applications with deficiencies in documentation for student grants corresponding to the 2025-2026 academic year, specifically for the Línia 2 CAIB, which is intended for university degree and master's studies outside the island. Applicants must review the list published on the Consell's Electronic Headquarters to check if their application has any deficiencies that need to be corrected. The list includes 154 applications that require amendment, of which 148 correspond to university degree studies and 6 to master's studies. The detected deficiencies refer to the need to accredit that a place could not be obtained to study in Ibiza or to justify documentally that no other aid or subsidy was received during the 2025-2026 academic year.

## Next Steps
Applicants with deficiencies in their documentation have a deadline of ten working days from the day after the publication of the notice to provide the required documentation. This procedure can be done through the Consell's Electronic Headquarters or in person at the General Registry of Entry, among other enabled registries. The Consell emphasizes the importance of reviewing this information and submitting the documentation within the established deadline, as failure to do so will result in the applicant being considered to have withdrawn their application. This list of amendments corresponds exclusively to Línia 2 CAIB, as Línia 1 of the Consell Insular d'Eivissa has already been resolved and is not part of this procedure.
