## General Strike on November 11
The Consell Insular d'Eivissa (Ibiza Island Council) has established minimum services that will be in place on November 11, during the general strike called at the state level. This ensures the continuity of essential public services, particularly those related to vulnerable people, security, and emergencies.

## Essential Services
The council will maintain essential services at the Social Welfare office, Minor Protection, Women's Office, Women's Shelter, residential centers, and the Hospital Residència Assistida de Cas Serres. Minimum services will also be guaranteed at the Vehicle Inspection Center (ITV), the Registry Assistance Office, road infrastructure, infant education, public facility maintenance, and services related to security and emergencies.

## Public Transport
The bus network will operate with minimum services to ensure essential mobility and connections between municipalities, population centers, and strategic points on the island. The main lines (T1, T2, T3, T4, T5, T6, and T7) will have distributed services throughout the day. The most in-demand services will have the following schedules: T1 (Ibiza-Sant Rafel-Sant Antoni) will run from early morning to midnight, with more frequency during peak hours.

## Planning Your Journey
The council recommends checking the schedules before starting your journey and planning your trips in advance. A complete document with all the lines, expeditions, and declared minimum service schedules is available on the council's website. The expeditions marked in yellow in the document are the declared minimum services and must be complied with. According to the Consell, "les expedicions marcades en groc" are the minimum services that will be in place.
