## Introduction to CEPCA
The Consell d'Eivissa (Ibiza Council) has commemorated the 25th anniversary of the Centre d'Estudis i Prevenció de Conductes Addictives (CEPCA), a service that began operating on 12 November 2001. The centre has evolved to address new challenges faced by young people, from drug consumption to the use of technology, equality, and emotional health. According to the Consell d'Eivissa, 80% of Ibiza's students have received some form of preventive session from CEPCA, and seven out of ten young people acknowledge that these interventions have had some influence on them.

## Evolution and Impact
Since its creation in 2001, CEPCA has adapted to the changing needs of young people on the island. Initially, the centre focused on preventing drug consumption and other addictive behaviours, but over the years, it has expanded its programmes to address new realities affecting childhood and adolescence. This evolution has been possible due to continuous study and observation of the island's social reality, allowing the centre to adapt its interventions to new risks and needs. The president of the Consell d'Eivissa, Vicent Marí, highlighted the centre's trajectory, saying "Invertir en prevenció és invertir en salut" (Investing in prevention is investing in health).

## Current Work and Future Plans
Currently, CEPCA develops continuous educational interventions that begin in primary school and accompany students throughout their secondary education. The centre also offers training activities for families, individualised advice, studies on adolescent habits and behaviour, and awareness campaigns. The Consell d'Eivissa has reinforced CEPCA with an expanded team and resources, allowing the centre to assume new functions related to sensitivity programmes on equality and diversity. The centre will continue to focus on prevention based on evidence, observation of the reality of young people on the island, and continuous intervention, with the objective of consolidating the trend of reducing substance consumption in early ages.

## Recognition and Appreciation
The president of the Consell d'Eivissa has dedicated special recognition to the professionals who have been part of CEPCA over the past 25 years, including the centre's coordinator, Belén Alvite, and the team that initiated the project. Marí expressed his gratitude for their work and commitment, stating that CEPCA is the result of the efforts of many people who believe in the importance of prevention. The Consell d'Eivissa's celebration of CEPCA's 25th anniversary highlights the council's commitment to investing in the health, wellbeing, and future of the island's young people.
