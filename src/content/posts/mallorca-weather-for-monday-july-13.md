Mallorca faces another very hot week. Aemet has issued an amber warning for the interior of the island, where temperatures are expected to reach around 39°C, and a yellow warning for the rest of Mallorca except the Serra de Tramuntana, with highs near 37°C.

## The week ahead

- The amber warning for the interior continues into Tuesday.
- Heat warnings are likely to remain in place until at least Saturday.
- Thursday could be the hottest day, with up to 42°C possible inland — close to the threshold for a red warning.

## Why it is so hot

A mass of hot air is moving north from Africa, bringing not only high temperatures but also suspended dust, which can give the sky a hazy, milky appearance. Humidity, which had been unusually low, is rising, making the heat feel more oppressive, particularly in the afternoon.

Winds will be mostly light, with sea breezes along the coasts in the afternoon.

## Sunday's readings

The highest temperatures on Sunday were 38.4°C in Marratxí and Sant Elm. The UV index reached 9, which is very high.

## Staying safe

- Drink water regularly, even if you are not thirsty.
- Wear light, loose clothing, a hat and sunglasses.
- Avoid outdoor activity in the middle of the day; early morning or after 8pm is best.
- Take breaks in the shade or in air-conditioned places.
- Watch for signs of heat exhaustion — dizziness, headache, heavy sweating, nausea — and call 112 if symptoms are severe.
