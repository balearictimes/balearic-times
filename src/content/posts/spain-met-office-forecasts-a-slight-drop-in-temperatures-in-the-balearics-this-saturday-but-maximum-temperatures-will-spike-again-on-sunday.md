Aemet expects a slight drop in temperatures across the Balearics on Saturday, but the heat will return on Sunday, with highs of up to 39°C in parts of Mallorca.

## Friday: still very hot

- Amber warning for high temperatures across most of Mallorca, except the east.
- Yellow warning for the rest of the islands.
- Very warm nights, with minimums of 19–25°C.

## Saturday: a small break

- Yellow warnings from midday in the interior, south and Serra de Tramuntana of Mallorca, and in Ibiza.
- Maximums of 31–38°C, slightly lower than on Friday.
- Partly cloudy skies with medium and high cloud, and possible early morning fog patches.
- Light easterly winds.

## Sunday: heat returns

- Yellow warnings continue in the same areas of Mallorca.
- Temperatures rise again, reaching around 39°C in places.
- Minimums of 20–25°C.
- High cloud and some suspended dust.
- Light to moderate east to north-easterly winds, with stronger gusts on the northern slopes of the Tramuntana.

## Tips for the weekend

- **Drink regularly** and carry water with you.
- **Plan outdoor activities** for the morning or evening.
- **Avoid intense exercise** in the middle of the day when warnings are in force.
- **Eat light,** choosing water-rich foods such as watermelon, cucumber and salads.
- **Keep your home cool:** close blinds during the day and ventilate at night.
- **Check Aemet's warnings,** which can change from day to day.

## Warm nights

The warm nights are one of the most tiring aspects of a long heatwave, making it harder to sleep and recover. Fans, cool showers before bed and keeping bedrooms dark during the day can help. Older people living alone should be checked on regularly.
