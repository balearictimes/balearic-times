## Introduction to Eco-Schools
The Consell d'Eivissa's schools, Cas Serres, Es Fameliar, and Ses Païsses, have received recognition for their commitment to environmental education and sustainability. The award ceremony took place at the Universitat de les Illes Balears (UIB) in Ibiza and Formentera, where the schools were presented with certificates and awards for their participation in the Centres Ecoambientals programme.

## Environmental Initiatives
The three schools have integrated sustainability into their educational projects, offering various activities and spaces that promote experiential learning and respect for the environment. Some of the initiatives include ecological gardens, composters, chicken coops, fish ponds, hydroponic projects, and lizard refuges. Additionally, the schools organise activities related to recycling, reusing materials, and reducing waste. These initiatives allow children to experiment with natural cycles, observe biodiversity, and develop sustainable habits from a young age.

## Recognition and Commitment
The director insular, Miquel Costa Tur, stated that "this recognition is the result of the involvement of educational teams, children, and their families, who actively participate in projects that contribute to creating greater environmental awareness from early childhood". He also emphasized that "the Consell's schools will continue to commit to an education that prioritizes sustainability and respect for our environment". With this distinction, the three schools consolidate their participation in the Centres Ecoambientals network of the Balearic Islands, a programme that promotes the incorporation of environmental and sustainability criteria into the daily life of educational centres.

## Future Developments
The recognition received by Cas Serres, Es Fameliar, and Ses Païsses is a testament to the Consell d'Eivissa's commitment to environmental education and sustainability. As the schools continue to develop their eco-friendly projects, they will likely serve as a model for other educational institutions in the region, promoting a culture of sustainability and respect for the environment among children and families in Ibiza.
