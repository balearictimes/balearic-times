## Free workshop for club administrators

The Consell d'Eivissa (Ibiza Island Council) will hold a training session on Friday 30 October aimed at strengthening the management of the island's sports clubs. The three-hour workshop, organised by the council's Department of Sports, runs from 17:00 to 20:00 in the plenary hall (sala de plens) of the Consell building. Admission is free until the venue reaches capacity.

## Expert speaker on sports law

The session will be led by Javier Hurlé, a specialist in sports law and the management of sports entities. Hurlé holds a degree in Law and Labour Relations from the University of Oviedo and postgraduate qualifications in sports law, sports entity management, sports marketing and social security. He has experience delivering training to clubs, directors and technical staff on sports legislation, the rights and obligations of sports entities, and the legal protection of minors in sport.

## Focus on day-to-day club operations

The workshop is designed for board members, management officers, coordinators and anyone involved in the running of a sports club. According to the Consell, the goal is to support the work that goes on beyond training sessions and competitions, providing practical knowledge for the daily organisation and administration of these entities.

## Practical information

- **Date:** Friday 30 October
- **Time:** 17:00–20:00
- **Venue:** Sala de plens, Consell d'Eivissa
- **Cost:** Free entry (limited capacity)
- **Target audience:** Club board members, managers, coordinators and related personnel

No prior registration is mentioned; attendees will be admitted on a first-come, first-served basis.
