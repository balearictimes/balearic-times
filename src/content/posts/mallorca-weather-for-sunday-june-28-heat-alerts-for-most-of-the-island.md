Aemet has extended Sunday's yellow heat warnings, which initially covered only the south and interior of Mallorca, to include the Serra de Tramuntana and the north and north-east. The warnings run from midday to 8pm, with the highest temperatures, up to 38°C, expected in the south and interior. Skies will be sunny, with some dust in the air.

The UV index will reach 10, which is extreme.

## Saturday's readings

The highest temperatures on Saturday were 37.5°C in Llucmajor, 37.3°C in Binissalem and 36.8°C in Santa Maria.

## The week ahead

There are no warnings for Monday. Temperatures will ease slightly during the week but stay above 30°C — still above the normal maximum of around 28–30°C for early July.

The chance of rain has increased for Thursday, with some possibility of showers on Wednesday too. From Monday, winds will come mainly from the north and north-east, which could bring slightly fresher air.

## Tips for Sunday

- Carry water and drink regularly.
- Wear a hat and apply high-factor sun cream; with UV at 10, skin can burn in minutes.
- People with respiratory problems should take care with dust in the air.
- Avoid strenuous activity during the warning hours.

Early next week should feel a little more comfortable, though still warm enough for the beach. Keep a light waterproof handy for Wednesday and Thursday, just in case.
