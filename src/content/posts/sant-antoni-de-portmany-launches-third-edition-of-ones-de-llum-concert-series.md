## Introduction to Ones de Llum
The Culture Department of Sant Antoni de Portmany Town Hall has presented the third edition of Ones de Llum, a cycle of visual concerts that combine live music with video mapping designed specifically for the municipality's churches. After two successful editions, Ones de Llum returns this autumn with a programme of five concerts that showcase the musical talent of Ibiza and Formentera, offering the public an artistic experience that combines music, light, and architectural heritage.

## Concert Programme
The third edition of Ones de Llum will feature five concerts at different churches in Sant Antoni de Portmany. The first concert will take place on 30 October at 20:00 at the Church of Sant Mateu, with Faith Converters, a project that combines different musical languages and scenic elements. The subsequent concerts will be held on 21 November at the Church of Sant Rafel, 19 December at the Church of Can Bonet, 23 January at the Church of Sant Antoni, and 20 February at the Church of Santa Agnès. Each concert will feature a unique combination of music and video mapping, with the visual proposal designed by Jesús de las Heras, of El Imaginario.

## Ticket Management
The management of tickets will be done in a mixed manner, with 50% of the places available for online reservation through a registration form on the Town Hall's communication channels, and the other 50% reserved for in-person collection at the church, one hour before the start of each concert.

## Cultural Significance
According to the Culture Councillor, Eva Prats, "Ones de Llum is a proposal that combines music, light, and heritage in emblematic spaces like the municipality's churches, while giving visibility to the musical talent of Ibiza and Formentera". The initiative aims to dynamise cultural activity during the autumn and winter months, bringing diverse musical proposals to the resident public and promoting local talent. The cycle is driven by the Culture Department of Sant Antoni de Portmany Town Hall, produced by Eivissa Escènica, and features the collaboration of Jesús de las Heras, of El Imaginario, responsible for the video mapping creations. As Carlos Vesperinas, director of Eivissa Escènica, said, "each church will offer a different proposal, both musically and visually".
