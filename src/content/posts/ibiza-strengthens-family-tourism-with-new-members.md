## Introduction to Ibiza Family Moments
The Consell Insular d'Eivissa has strengthened its Ibiza Family Moments project with the addition of 32 new members, 12 of which have been awarded the star category. This project, which currently boasts 15,849 places and 177 public and private products, aims to promote family tourism on the island.

## The Project's Objectives
According to the Consell, Ibiza Family Moments is not just a new segment to be created from scratch, but rather a consolidation of the island's existing family tourism offerings. The president of the Consell, Vicent Marí, stated that "the tourism family is not a new segment that we have to create from zero". The project's objective is to recover the prominence of family tourism, make visible the existing offerings, and work with businesses to generate real opportunities.

## Current Status and Future Plans
The Consell has shared the preliminary conclusions of a study on family tourism, which reveals that approximately one in four visitors travels to Ibiza with their family. The study also found that 58% of family visitors repeat their destination, and the average satisfaction with their holidays is 87 out of 100. To build on these findings, the Consell plans to focus on four priority areas in 2027: promoting member businesses, expanding services and tools, connecting businesses to generate new experiences, and improving market knowledge and technical support.

## Next Steps
The Consell will continue to promote and coordinate the Ibiza Family Moments project, but its success will depend on the participation of businesses and their ability to create new proposals. As Vicent Marí concluded, "Ibiza Family Moments has to be a living, useful network capable of generating more collaboration and more opportunities". The Consell will work on initiatives such as creating a public-private working group, a coordinated agenda of family activities, a specialization guide for businesses, and a catalog of experiences to achieve these goals.
