## Introduction to Inclusive Sport
The Consell d'Eivissa, Ibiza's island council, has organised an inclusive sport event for 48 students from CEIP Portal Nou school, as part of its educational programme DIE - Esport Inclusiu a l'Escola. The event, which took place at the school, featured a talk by paralympic judoka Marta Arce Payno, followed by practical activities in various adapted sports disciplines.

## The Event
The event began with a talk by Marta Arce, who shared her personal and sporting experience with the students. She spoke about the challenges she has faced throughout her career and the values of perseverance and self-improvement that sport has given her. The students were able to ask questions and interact with Marta in a participatory atmosphere. The practical part of the event took place at the school's sports facilities and the des Pratet poliesportiu, where the students tried out different adapted sports, including judo for people with visual impairments, sitting volleyball, goalball, and athletics for people with physical and visual disabilities.

## The Programme
The event is part of the Consell d'Eivissa's programme to promote inclusive sport in schools. The programme, which is financed by the council's sports department, aims to bring adapted sport to educational centres, raise awareness about the reality of people with disabilities, and promote values such as equality of opportunities, respect, cooperation, and self-improvement. The programme is co-organised with the Cátedra Fundación Sanitas de Estudios sobre Deporte Inclusivo (CEDI) and has the collaboration of Eivissa town hall.

## Conclusion
The Consell d'Eivissa's commitment to promoting inclusive sport and education based on participation, coexistence, and equality of opportunities is reinforced by this event. By bringing inclusive sport to schools, the council aims to make sport more accessible to everyone, regardless of their abilities. As the council said, the programme aims to "acostar l’esport adaptat als centres educatius" (bring adapted sport to educational centres).
