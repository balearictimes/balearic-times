## Introduction to the Change
The Consell d'Eivissa has announced that the musical performance of Coco, el musical, initially scheduled for Saturday, October 31, at the Recinte Firal d'Eivissa, has been rescheduled to Sunday, November 1, at 5:00 pm. This decision comes as a result of the Spanish Government's declaration of three days of official mourning following the tragic train accident in Toledo.

## Details of the Rescheduling
According to the Consell d'Eivissa, individuals who have already purchased tickets for the Saturday performance can use them to attend the Sunday show at 5:00 pm without needing to make any changes or additional arrangements. Additionally, there will be two performances of Coco, el musical on Sunday, November 1: the previously scheduled show at 12:00 pm, which is sold out, and the new performance at 5:00 pm, for which tickets are still available.

## Refund Option and Alternative Event
For those who cannot attend the rescheduled performance, the Consell d'Eivissa has set up an email address, cij.eivissa@conselldeivissa.es, where they can request a refund for their ticket purchase. The musical, inspired by the animated film, combines music, dance, and a colorful stage setting to tell a story about family, memories, and Mexican traditions surrounding the Day of the Dead.

## Suspension of Another Event
The Consell d'Eivissa also announced the suspension of the II Trobada de Corals Lina Bufí, which was scheduled to take place on Saturday, October 31, at 8:00 pm, at the church of Sant Antoni de Portmany, due to the same reason. The event was supposed to bring together several choirs, including the Coral Parroquial de Sant Antoni, the Cor de ses Figueretes, the Cor de l’Església Catòlica Grega Ucraïnesa, and the Cor Ciutat d’Eivissa. The Consell d'Eivissa expressed its condolences and solidarity with the victims of the train accident in Toledo, their families, and all those affected.
