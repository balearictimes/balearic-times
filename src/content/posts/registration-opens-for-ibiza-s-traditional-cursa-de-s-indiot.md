## Introduction to the Event
The Consell Insular d'Eivissa has opened registration for the traditional Cursa de s'Indiot, a prominent cycling event in Ibiza's calendar, taking place on Sunday, 13 December. The event will maintain its traditional route, starting and finishing at the avenue of Santa Eulària des Riu in Ibiza, near the Burger King.

## Registration Details
Registration for the event is free and can be done through the website https://gosportmadness.com/cursa-sindiot-2026/ until 12 December or until all available places are filled. The competitive race has a maximum of 175 places, while the Indiot Mini, a non-competitive event, has 75 places. According to the Conseller d'Esports, Salva Losa, "tornar a convocar la Cursa de s'Indiot" is a commitment to the essence of cycling on the island.

## Event Details
The competitive race is approximately 42 kilometres long and is open to participants aged 15 and above. The race will start at 9 am, with the first two kilometres neutralised. Participants must use a road bike and a homologated helmet. The event includes various categories, including cadet, junior, and master categories. The Indiot Mini, which will take place after the competitive race, is open to children under 15 and people with reduced mobility or special needs. This event will take place on a 1.4-kilometre circuit at the same avenue.

## Participation and Safety
Registration is personal and non-transferable, and participants under 18 must have authorisation from a parent or guardian. Participants can collect their race numbers and chips on 12 December at the des Raspallar sports complex or on the day of the event. All participants must respect traffic regulations and follow the organiser's instructions, as the event takes place on open roads. More information about the event can be found on the website or by emailing eventos@sportmadness.club.
