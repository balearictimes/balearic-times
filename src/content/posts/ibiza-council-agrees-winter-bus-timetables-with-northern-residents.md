## Introduction to Winter Bus Timetables
The Consell d'Eivissa has agreed on the winter bus timetables with residents of the northern part of the island. The new timetables, which will come into effect on November 1, include modifications to improve connections between Ibiza and the northern towns.

## Modifications to Bus Services
According to the Consell d'Eivissa, the modifications will affect connections to Sant Miquel, Port de Sant Miquel, Portinatx, and Sant Joan. The changes include the maintenance of evening services on two of the lines that connect Ibiza with the north of the island. Specifically, the 21:30 services on the T6 and A9 lines will be maintained, allowing users to continue to have a public transport service in the evening during the winter season.

## Improvements for Students and Night-Time Services
Another improvement is the adaptation of a service to facilitate the return of students from the IES Balàfia after classes. The bus will leave Portinatx at 13:40, pass through Sant Joan at 13:55, and arrive at the Sant Llorenç stop at 14:06, coinciding with the end of classes. Additionally, a pilot night bus service, the N3 line, will be introduced, connecting Ibiza with Santa Gertrudis, Sant Miquel, Port de Sant Miquel, Portinatx, Sant Joan, and Sant Llorenç. This service will operate on Friday, Saturday, and eve of public holiday nights, with four departures from the Ibiza bus station.

## Implementation and Evaluation
The Consell d'Eivissa said that the new timetables will be available on the Alsa website. Mariano Juan, the vice-president and councillor for Mobility, stated that "with this pilot test we want to offer a night-time mobility alternative to residents of the north of the island, especially young people, so they can travel without needing to use private vehicles." The Consell will evaluate the functioning of the new services and determine the needs of the users. The three improvements will come into effect on November 1, coinciding with the implementation of the new winter timetables.
