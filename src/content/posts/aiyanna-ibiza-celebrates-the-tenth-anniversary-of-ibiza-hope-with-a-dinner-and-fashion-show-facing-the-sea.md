Ibiza Hope, the fashion label created on the island by designer Ivonne Carolina, is celebrating its tenth anniversary with a fashion show and dinner at Aiyanna Ibiza, the beachfront restaurant at Cala Nova, on 18 July.

## The brand

Ibiza Hope makes feminine, relaxed clothing inspired by the island's light, its sense of freedom and its bohemian heritage. Ivonne Carolina describes the label as a way of telling stories through fashion, inspired by Ibiza, its women and its particular mix of freedom and elegance. Her designs are aimed at women who want to feel authentic and confident.

## The venue

Aiyanna, on Cala Nova beach in the municipality of Santa Eulària des Riu, is known for Mediterranean dishes designed for sharing and a relaxed atmosphere that runs from breakfast through to evening dinners overlooking the sea. The restaurant's emphasis on nature and an unhurried island lifestyle makes it a natural partner for the brand.

## The evening

- **The show.** A selection of Ibiza Hope's latest designs will be presented on a catwalk facing the sea, timed for the early evening light.
- **The dinner.** A menu created for the occasion, built around local ingredients.
- **The setting.** As the sun goes down over Cala Nova, the event is designed to capture the atmosphere that inspires the brand.

## Full moon dinner

Aiyanna's summer programme continues on 29 July with one of its popular full moon dinners, served on the beach under the moonlight.

## Practical information

- **Date:** 18 July
- **Place:** Aiyanna Ibiza, Cala Nova, Santa Eulària des Riu
- **Booking:** places are limited and must be reserved in advance through the restaurant

Ten years is a significant milestone for an independent fashion label, and Ibiza Hope's anniversary reflects the strength of the island's homegrown fashion scene, which since the days of Adlib has drawn on Ibiza's light, colour and relaxed style.
