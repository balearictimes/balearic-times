## New Green Space for Residents
Ibiza Town Council is set to approve the creation of a new 3,273m² green zone in the Es Llimoners-Sa Real neighbourhood, fulfilling a commitment made to local residents. The council will submit a proposal to modify the town's urban planning rules at its next meeting, allowing the area to be reclassified as a public green space.

## Background and Context
The new green zone is the result of a participatory process with local residents, who expressed a desire for a traditional-style green space with vegetation, shaded areas, and spaces for walking and relaxation. The project aims to improve the quality of life for residents in the area and create a more pleasant urban environment. According to the Mayor of Ibiza, Rafael Triguero, "aquest és un compromís" (this is a commitment) made to the residents of Es Llimoners-Sa Real.

## The Project
The modification of the urban planning rules will allow the area to be incorporated into the town's network of public green spaces, creating a green connection between the Abel Matutes boulevard and the Joan Marí Cardona park. The new green zone will be designed with the needs of local residents in mind, prioritizing daily use by families, older people, and the community as a whole. The project will increase the town's total green space from 866,645m² to 869,918m².

## Next Steps
The proposal will be submitted to the council for initial approval on October 26, after which it will undergo a period of public consultation and review by relevant authorities before final approval. The Ibiza Town Council has reaffirmed its commitment to working with residents to improve public spaces, increase green areas, and create a more sustainable and habitable town.
