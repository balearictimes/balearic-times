## Introduction to the Project
The Ajuntament de Sant Antoni de Portmany (Sant Antoni Town Hall) has commenced a comprehensive renovation of the municipal football stadium's grandstands this month. The project, valued at 1,846,878.55 euros, is expected to be completed within eight months. According to the Ajuntament, the renovation aims to recover and modernise one of the municipality's primary sports infrastructures, ensuring safety, improving functionality, and adapting it to current regulations.

## Scope of the Renovation
The renovation project includes structural reinforcement of the grandstands, repair and renovation of deteriorated elements, waterproofing work, and rehabilitation of the cafeteria's structure. Additionally, the project involves adapting the facilities to meet the current requirements of the Technical Building Code (CTE), including improvements in accessibility and fire safety, as well as the renovation of electrical, telecommunications, water, sanitation, ventilation, gas, and sound installations.

## Background and Necessity
The need for a comprehensive renovation arose from the detection of significant structural deficiencies in the stadium, which was built between 1987 and 1988. The Ajuntament, in collaboration with the municipality's football clubs, analysed various alternatives and opted for the most comprehensive solution, involving the integral rehabilitation of the grandstands and changing rooms. This renovation will enable the restoration of safety conditions, improve the functionality of the stadium, and adapt the facility to current regulations, making it a suitable space for sports practice in Sant Antoni.

## Expected Outcomes
Upon completion, the renovated stadium is expected to provide a safer and more functional environment for users. The Ajuntament's investment in this project demonstrates its commitment to maintaining and improving the municipality's sports infrastructure, ultimately benefiting the local community and football clubs that utilise the facility. With the renovation underway, the municipality is poised to enhance its sports offerings and provide a better experience for athletes and spectators alike.
