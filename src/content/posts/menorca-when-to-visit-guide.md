Menorca is a smaller, calmer island than its neighbours, and its season is shorter. Understanding the calendar helps you decide when to go, because the experience at the end of August is quite different from the one in March.

## The main season

The tourist season runs roughly from May to October. In July and August the island is busy, hotel prices are at their highest and the south coast car parks fill early. Daytime temperatures often reach the high twenties or low thirties Celsius, and the sea is at its warmest. Sunbeds, boat trips and restaurants are all open, though reservations are wise.

## The best months for balance

June and September are generally the favourite months for visitors who want warm weather and more space. The sea is pleasant for swimming, the evenings are long and the roads are quieter. In June, Ciutadella holds its Sant Joan fiesta, which draws crowds and fills accommodation, so book early if you plan to go.

May can be a good month too, with warm days, flowers on the hillsides and lower prices, though the water is still cool.

## October

October has plenty of warm days, especially early in the month, but the weather is less predictable. Some businesses begin to close at the end of the month, and ferry and flight schedules are reduced.

## Winter

Between November and March many hotels, beach bars and restaurants close, and flights and ferries are less frequent. The island is quiet, green and good for walking, but the weather can be wet and cold. A strong northerly wind called the tramontana blows on some days and can be fierce. Mahón and Ciutadella stay open through the year, with local shops and cafés, so you can still find places to eat.

## Spring

March and April bring wildflowers and mild days. The Camí de Cavalls is a popular walk at this time, and the island is peaceful. The sea is cold, so it is a time for hiking and cycling rather than for the beach.

## Rough guide

- **Best for beaches**: June to September.
- **Best for walking and cycling**: April, May, late September and October.
- **Best for quiet**: November to March, with limited services.
- **Best for festivals**: late June for Sant Joan in Ciutadella, and late summer for other village fiestas.
- **Cheapest**: outside July and August, especially in spring and autumn.

## Wind and weather

Menorca is windier than the other islands, and a northerly wind can make the north coast beaches uncomfortable for a few days. The south coast is usually more sheltered. Check the forecast and be ready to switch sides if the weather changes. It is one of the reasons a hire car is useful.

## A year in Menorca

January and February are quiet, with cool, windy days and green hills. Sant Antoni fiestas in January see bonfires in some villages. In March the almond trees flower and the first walkers arrive. Easter is a major festival, with processions in Ciutadella and Mahón.

April and May see the tourist season begin. Hotels reopen, boat trips restart and the Camí de Cavalls is at its best, with wildflowers on the verges. The sea is cool but swimmable for the brave at the end of May.

June brings the Sant Joan fiesta in Ciutadella on 23 and 24 June, when riders on black Menorcan horses parade through the streets, and the horses rear up before crowds in the main square, Plaça des Born. The fiesta draws large crowds, so book early.

July and August are the main months. Villages including Es Castell, Es Mercadal, Sant Lluís and Alaior hold their own fiestas through the summer. The sea is warm, at around 25°C, and the south coast coves are busy.

September is a gentle month. The Mahón fiestas of La Mare de Déu de Gràcia are held in the first week. The sea remains warm, and the island is calmer. In October, the light is softer, some days are wet and many tourist businesses close at the end of the month.

November and December are quiet. The tramontana wind often blows. Christmas sees traditional markets and festivals, and the Menorcan cuisine turns to hearty stews.

## Weather facts

Menorca receives around 300 days of sun a year, but it is windier than the other islands. The tramontana blows from the north in winter, and the mestral can blow in spring. Average summer highs are around 29°C, and the sea temperature peaks in August. Rain falls mostly in autumn and early winter.

## What is open

Most beach bars and many restaurants close between November and April. Mahón and Ciutadella have year-round shops and restaurants. Ferry and flight schedules are reduced from late October, so plan connections carefully. The island council keeps a list of open hotels in winter.

## Events to plan around

- **Easter**: processions in Ciutadella and Mahón.
- **23 and 24 June**: Sant Joan in Ciutadella.
- **Late July**: Sant Jaume fiestas in Es Castell, and the Sant Cristòfol fiestas in Es Mercadal.
- **Early September**: Gràcia fiestas in Mahón.

## Choosing by interest

For beaches, the best time is June to September. For walkers and cyclists, April to June and September to November are more comfortable. For birdwatchers, spring and autumn migrations bring many species to S'Albufera des Grau. For photographers, the soft light of October and March suits the lighthouses and the harbours.

## Costs and bookings

Prices rise sharply in July and August. Book ferries, hire cars and the best hotels early. In June and September, prices are lower and the island is easier to enjoy. In winter, bargains are available, but you need a car and a flexible approach.

## Official information

The Consell Insular de Menorca publishes events calendars and visitor information. AEMET, at aemet.es, gives weather forecasts and warnings.
