## Introduction to Ibiza Revelada
The Consell d'Eivissa has presented "Ibiza Revelada", a new tourism initiative, to 15 UK travel agents in London. According to the Consell, this project aims to showcase the island's historical, cultural, and natural heritage by connecting 98 points of interest.

## The Project's Structure
The initiative is structured around four main themes: defensive and coastal Ibiza, rural and traditional Ibiza, social and cultural Ibiza, and archaeological Ibiza. These themes cover various aspects of the island, including its architecture, history, art, and traditions. The project combines physical materials, such as maps, with digital content that allows visitors to delve deeper into the island's history and significance.

## Objective and Target Audience
The objective of Ibiza Revelada, as stated by the Consell, is to help visitors understand the island's heritage and its importance to the local community. The project targets both new and repeat visitors, offering them new reasons to explore the island and its different towns and municipalities. As Vicent Marí, the president of the Consell, said, the goal is to "comprendre allò que veuen" (understand what they see).

## Future Plans and Tourism Model
The Consell plans to incorporate QR codes at points of interest, allowing visitors to access more information via their mobile devices. The project's content is currently available in Spanish and English, with plans to add more languages in the future. The initiative is part of the Consell's efforts to promote a more sustainable tourism model, reducing pressure on the island's territory and public services during peak months while encouraging visitors to explore the island during other times of the year. With its strong connection to the UK market, the Consell hopes that Ibiza Revelada will contribute to a better understanding and appreciation of the island's unique identity and heritage.
