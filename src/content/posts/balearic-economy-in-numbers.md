This page collects the main official numbers about the Balearic economy in one place, with the source and date for each. It is meant for readers who want facts they can check. All figures come from official bodies: the Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), the regional statistics office IBESTAT, the Balearic tourism agency (AETIB), the Consell Econòmic i Social (CES), and the Ministry of Inclusion and Social Security. Where a figure is provisional, it is marked as such. The page was last checked on 29 October 2026, and later releases will replace some of these numbers.

## Output and income

- **Gross value added growth in 2024:** 4 per cent (CES, Memoria 2024).
- **Nominal GDP per person in 2024:** 34,381 euros, up 10.9 per cent, the largest rise in Spain (CES).
- **Share of services in economic activity:** 85.8 per cent (CES).

## Jobs

- **Average affiliation to Social Security in 2024:** 607,250, up 3.0 per cent, a historical high (CES).
- **Jobs by sector, 2024:** services 81.6 per cent, construction 10.5 per cent, industry 7.2 per cent, primary 0.7 per cent (CES).
- **Seasonal swing:** affiliation in July 2024 was 34.9 per cent above December (CES).
- **Workers in tourism, fourth quarter of 2025:** 94,706 affiliates, up 3.5 per cent, or 19.1 per cent of all affiliates (AETIB).
- **Tourism businesses registered, fourth quarter of 2025:** 8,825, up 1.3 per cent (AETIB).
- **Temporary contract rate in tourism, fourth quarter of 2025:** 13.6 per cent (AETIB).

## Unemployment

- **Average unemployed, 2024:** 33,980, down 6.7 per cent (CES).
- **Unemployment rate, 2024 average:** 8.2 per cent, the lowest since 2007 (CES).
- **Unemployment rate, third quarter of 2026:** 4.63 per cent, the lowest of any region (INE, Encuesta de Población Activa).
- **Spain, third quarter of 2026:** 10.18 per cent (INE).

## Wages and labour cost

- **Average labour cost per worker, 2024:** 2,254.9 euros per month, 2.0 per cent below the national average and 4.8 per cent above 2023 (CES).
- **National minimum wage 2026:** 1,221 euros per month in 14 payments, 17,094 euros per year (Real Decreto 126/2026).

## Tourists

- **Tourists in 2024:** 18.7 million, up 4.9 per cent on 2023 and 13.7 per cent above 2019 (CES).
- **Tourist spending in 2024:** 22,381 million euros, up 12.2 per cent (CES).
- **Spending per tourist per day, 2024:** 187 euros, up 7.0 per cent (CES).
- **Hotel overnight stays, 2024:** up 7.1 per cent, with the average stay down 2.8 per cent (CES).
- **Tourists in August 2026:** 3,167,905, up 2.33 per cent on August 2025 (IBESTAT, provisional).
- **Tourists from the United Kingdom in August 2026:** 649,101, or 20.49 per cent of the total, up 10.45 per cent (IBESTAT).
- **Tourists from Germany in August 2026:** 639,022, or 20.17 per cent, up 0.47 per cent (IBESTAT).
- **Tourists from the rest of Spain in August 2026:** 571,447, or 18.04 per cent, up 1.29 per cent (IBESTAT).
- **Tourists from January to August 2026:** 14,357,229, up 1.89 per cent on the same period of 2025 (IBESTAT). Over the same period, UK visitors rose 6.96 per cent and German visitors fell 1.05 per cent.
- **January 2026:** 244,275 tourists, down 11.7 per cent, spending 265.1 million euros, up 0.3 per cent. Spending was 130.31 euros per person per day (AETIB).

## Accommodation

- **Open hotel places in January 2026:** 22,909 (an estimate), with 36.6 per cent occupancy (AETIB).
- **Rural tourism places in January 2026:** 2,059, with 28.8 per cent occupancy (AETIB).

The winter figures show how far the islands slow down in the low season. Many hotels close, and the open ones are less than 40 per cent full.

## Population

- **Growth in the second quarter of 2026:** the INE's provisional continuous population statistics, with data to 1 July 2026, show the Illes Balears growing 0.36 per cent in the quarter. Spain's growth was 0.21 per cent, to a total of 49,801,559 inhabitants.
- The exact population count of the islands is published by IBESTAT and the INE in the municipal and regional tables. The press note this article used did not give the Balearic total, so the number is not repeated here.

## Taxes and prices

- **Value added tax:** general rate 21 per cent, reduced rates 10 per cent and 4 per cent (Agencia Tributaria).
- **Corporate tax:** general rate 25 per cent. New companies pay 15 per cent in 2026. Small companies with turnover below one million euros pay 19 per cent on the first 50,000 euros of taxable profit and 21 per cent on the rest in 2026 (Agencia Tributaria).

## How to read these numbers

Three cautions apply. First, annual averages hide the seasonal peaks, and summer figures look better than winter ones. Second, tourist spending figures are estimates from surveys, so they carry error margins. Third, some series have different definitions. The CES uses annual averages of Social Security affiliation, while the INE labour survey measures people by interview and gives quarterly rates.

## Where to find the latest data

- IBESTAT, ibestat.es, for regional statistics.
- INE, ine.es, for the labour force survey, population and prices.
- AETIB, the tourism statistics pages on caib.es, for tourism.
- CES, caib.es/sites/ces, for the annual report.

## Questions readers ask

**Why do two sources give different unemployment rates?** One is the annual average estimated by the regional economic council, 8.2 per cent for 2024, and the other is the INE's labour survey for a single quarter, 4.63 per cent in the third quarter of 2026. The rates differ because of timing and method.

**Why do tourist numbers and spending move in different directions?** In January 2026, arrivals fell 11.7 per cent while spending rose 0.3 per cent, because each visitor spent more.

**How often do these numbers change?** Tourism figures are published monthly, labour survey data quarterly and the economic council's report annually.
