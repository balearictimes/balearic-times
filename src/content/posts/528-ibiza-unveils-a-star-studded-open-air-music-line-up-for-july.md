528 Ibiza, the open-air venue in the Benimussa hills near Sant Antoni, has announced its programme for July, with a mix of daytime parties, weekly residencies and a big closing night.

## Brunch! x The Gardens of Babylon

The month opens with two day-to-night parties combining music with yoga, art stalls and performances:

- **1 July:** Ben Böhmer, Jan Blomqvist, Flower and Unders.
- **8 July:** Monolink, Satori, Jamie and Romy Janssen.

## Thursdays: La Discothèque

Every Thursday from 2 to 30 July, La Discothèque presents five weeks of house, disco, soul and underground sounds, with:

- Colleen "Cosmo" Murphy;
- Annie Mac;
- Crazy P;
- Derrick Carter;
- DJ Paulette;
- Dan Shake;
- Antal back-to-back with Luke Una.

## Tuesdays: Pikes Presents

Pikes, the legendary Sant Antoni hotel, brings the spirit of Ibiza's club history to 528 on Tuesdays, with artists including 2manydjs, Groove Armada, Sasha & John Digweed, Erol Alkan, tINI and S.A.S.S. Nights start in the garden and move into the club later.

## Saturdays: Manumission's legacy

Andy Manumission, co-founder of one of the island's most famous parties, hosts Saturdays celebrating Ibiza's party heritage with a mix of veterans and newer names, including K-Klass, Doorly, Pete Gooding, Timo Maas, Yousef and Clara Da Costa.

## Other nights

Through the month, events from Search, JOY, Modern Function, Clockwork Orange and Bass Jamz cover everything from techno to bass music.

## BBC Radio 1 Dance — 31 July

July ends with a BBC Radio 1 Dance showcase featuring Disclosure, Gorgon City, Pete Tong, Danny Howard and Sarah Story.

## Why 528 stands out

Set in the countryside rather than in a superclub, 528 has carved out a place for itself with open-air events that mix established names, Ibiza veterans and a more relaxed atmosphere. Its programme leans heavily on the island's history, with brands and DJs closely tied to the golden years of Ibiza clubbing.
