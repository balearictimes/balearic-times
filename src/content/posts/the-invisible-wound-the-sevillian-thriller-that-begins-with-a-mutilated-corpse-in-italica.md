*La herida invisible* (The invisible wound) is a crime novel set in Seville that opens with a disturbing discovery: a mutilated body in the galleries of the Roman amphitheatre at Itálica, the ancient city on the outskirts of Seville.

## The story

The victim is a local tour guide who, months earlier, had been accused of assaulting a young Swedish tourist and had been cleared. Inspector Jara Vega is called to the scene. At first, everything points to revenge. But as she investigates, the case opens onto a much wider web of disappearances, hidden killings and dark alliances that leads her through the city's streets and into the depths of the internet.

## The detective

Jara Vega returns after her first appearance in a previous novel. In this new case, she must deal with conflicting testimony, unreliable memories and her own prejudices, while trying to stay objective as the investigation becomes more personal.

## Seville as a character

The city is much more than a backdrop. The novel uses Seville's streets, squares and history to build atmosphere, while Itálica — the birthplace of the emperors Trajan and Hadrian — adds a layer of ancient history to a contemporary crime.

## The themes

The book combines classic police procedure with current concerns:

- **the dark web**, and the anonymity it offers to criminals;
- **mental health**, and the way hidden suffering can drive people to extreme acts;
- **invisible wounds** — the emotional and psychological damage that leaves no visible scars, which gives the novel its title.

## Spain's crime fiction boom

The novel joins a strong tradition of Spanish crime fiction rooted in specific cities and regions, from the Basque Country to Galicia and Andalusia. Readers increasingly look for stories that combine suspense with a strong sense of place — and Seville, with its contrasts between monumental beauty and hidden corners, provides plenty of material.

For fans of police procedurals with an Andalusian setting, *La herida invisible* offers a fast-paced plot, a determined investigator and a city full of shadows.
