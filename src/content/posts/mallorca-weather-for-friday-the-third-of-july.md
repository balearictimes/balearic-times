Friday will be sunny across Mallorca but breezy, with a fresh to strong wind from the north or north-east. It is the calm before a hotter spell: the state meteorological agency, Aemet, says a mass of hot, dry air from Africa will push temperatures up over the coming days.

## The outlook

- **Sunday:** conditions begin to resemble a heatwave across the island.
- **Monday to Wednesday:** the hottest days, especially inland, with up to 38°C possible on Tuesday and Wednesday.
- **Later in the week:** a slight easing is possible, although some areas will stay hot.

A heatwave has not been formally declared, but the forecast points to several days of high temperatures.

Winds will be fresh to strong from the north-east in the morning in many areas, easing through the day.

## Thursday's readings

The highest temperatures on Thursday afternoon were in Sa Pobla (32.8°C), Llucmajor (32.7°C) and Santa Maria (32.4°C). The coolest overnight readings were in the mountains, with 14.2°C at Son Torrella in Escorca and 16.4°C at Lluc. The strongest gust, 67 km/h, was recorded at Capdepera.

## Tips for the coming days

- Drink water regularly, even if you are not thirsty.
- Use sun cream of at least SPF 30 and reapply it often; the breeze can make it feel cooler than it is.
- Secure parasols and towels on the beach while the wind is strong.
- Plan exercise and outdoor activities for the morning or evening.
