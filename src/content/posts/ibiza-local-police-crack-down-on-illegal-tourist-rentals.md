## Increased Controls
The Ibiza Local Police have intensified their efforts to combat illegal tourist rentals, with the fourth shift of the service issuing four new reports for alleged infractions related to this activity. According to the Ajuntament d'Eivissa, the police have also proposed two further sanctions for possible irregular tourist rental activities, based on witness statements.

## Action Against Intrusism
In addition to tackling illegal tourist rentals, the police have also been working to prevent intrusism in the transport of passengers. Between April 30 and October 16, there were 410 interventions to control transport in the municipality, resulting in the detection of illegal taxi services, irregular capture of clients, and other infringements of transport regulations. The proposed sanctions in one of the operations carried out during the summer reached 27,400 euros.

## Prevention and Control
The fourth shift of the police service has maintained a preventive presence in areas with high tourist traffic, such as the Marina, Port, and Figueretes, among others. This has involved carrying out security checks, alcohol and drug tests, monitoring public spaces, and ensuring compliance with municipal ordinances. On some occasions, the police have been accompanied by the canine unit. The Ajuntament d'Eivissa said that this complementary service has allowed the Local Police to expand their operational capacity during the tourist season, reinforcing their presence and tasks of prevention, inspection, and control to promote safety and coexistence between residents and visitors.

## Results So Far
Since the fourth shift of the service was launched in June, the police have documented at least 159 inspections of homes and issued 25 reports for possible infractions related to illegal tourist rentals, including the four new reports from last Sunday. The two new proposed sanctions are still pending processing. The Consell Insular has worked jointly with the Local Police to develop the devices that have made these actions possible. The "quart torn" service has reinforced the ordinary services of the Local Police during the periods of greatest tourist activity and at night.
