## Introduction to the Seminar
The Consell d'Eivissa, Ibiza's island council, is organising a seminar on the benefits of exercise for women's health, which will take place this Friday, 27 November, at the Recinte Firal d'Eivissa. The event, titled 'Dona, exercici físic i salut' (Women, Exercise, and Health), aims to raise awareness about the importance of physical activity and its adaptation to women's physiological characteristics at different stages of their lives.

## The Seminar Programme
The seminar will be led by Alberto García Bataller, a doctor in Sports Science and a renowned expert in women's training. The event will consist of two lectures, both open to the general public and professionals in the sports and health sectors, with free entry until the venue is full. The first lecture, 'Cicle menstrual, rendiment i salut' (Menstrual Cycle, Performance, and Health), will explore the different phases of the menstrual cycle and their impact on training and sports performance. It will also discuss the causes and prevention of common injuries in women and the issue of amenorrhea in sports.

## Practical Advice for Women's Health
The second lecture, 'Dona i 40 anys: comença el millor, si et cuides!' (Women and 40 Years: The Best is Yet to Come, If You Take Care of Yourself!), will focus on the physical, physiological, and emotional changes associated with perimenopause and menopause. García Bataller will explain how regular exercise can help reduce symptoms and prevent health problems such as osteoporosis, metabolic syndrome, and cardiovascular accidents. Both lectures will provide practical and accessible advice, offering tools and knowledge to women interested in improving their health and quality of life, as well as providing scientific information to professionals in the sports and health sectors.

## About the Speaker
Alberto García Bataller is a professor at the Universitat Politècnica de Madrid and a coordinator of the university's Equality Point. He is also the director of the university's expert course 'Dona, esport i salut' (Women, Sports, and Health) and the author of the book 'Mujeres en forma' (Women in Shape). With a notable background in high-performance sports, García Bataller has been a coach for the Spanish Olympic team at the Athens 2004 and Beijing 2008 Olympics. The seminar is an excellent opportunity for women and professionals to learn from an expert in the field and gain valuable insights into the importance of exercise for women's health.
