Rockstar has opened pre-orders for Grand Theft Auto VI in Spain and the rest of Europe, ahead of the game's launch on 19 November 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## Editions and prices

| Edition | Price |
|---|---|
| Standard Edition | €79.99 |
| Ultimate Edition | €99.99 |
| Upgrade from Standard to Ultimate | €20 |

Prices are the same on the PlayStation Store and the Microsoft Store. The Ultimate Edition includes an upgrade pack with extra content on top of the base game. Players who buy the Standard Edition can upgrade later for €20, so there is no penalty for waiting.

## Pre-order bonuses

Anyone who pre-orders or buys the game before 20 November receives:

**The Vintage Vice City Pack**, a nod to 2002's GTA: Vice City, including:

- a 1955 Vapid Stanier car;
- a garage on Ocean Drive;
- outfits and hairstyles inspired by Tommy Vercetti, the hero of Vice City;
- a weapon finish based on Tommy's famous Hawaiian shirt.

**One month of GTA+**, Rockstar's subscription service.

## No disc in the box

Physical copies will not contain a game disc. Instead, the box includes a download code. Boxed copies will start shipping on 12 November, a week before launch, so that players can pre-load the game and play as soon as it unlocks.

## Which to choose

For most players, the Standard Edition at €79.99 is the sensible choice, since the pre-order bonuses come with both editions and the Ultimate upgrade can be added later. Those who already know they want everything can buy the Ultimate Edition straight away.

GTA VI, set in the fictional state of Leonida, a version of Florida centred on Vice City, is one of the most anticipated games in years, following repeated delays to its release.
