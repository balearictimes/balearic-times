PORTSIDE Ibiza, the free open-air electronic music party in the Port of Ibiza, is returning this summer, bringing international DJs to the waterfront for a night that is open to everyone.

## Free for all

There is no entry fee. The event is aimed at residents, people working in the island's hospitality industry, visitors and music fans, and has become one of the few big electronic music nights in Ibiza that does not require a ticket.

This year's theme is "For Ibiza, With Love", a tribute to the island, its residents and those who keep its culture going all year round.

## The line-up

- **Luciano** returns for the fourth year running with his Latin-influenced house sets.
- **Bedouin** return after their SAGA party won Best House Party in Ibiza at the 2025 Vicious Awards.
- **Turquoise**, an Ibiza-based DJ and a regular at Chinois, represents the island's local scene.

## More than a party

The organisers say the event is intended to bring life to the harbour area and to thank the local institutions, businesses and residents who support the island's music scene. Bars, cafés and shops around the port also benefit from the crowds it draws.

## After-party at Chinois

When the music in the port ends, the night moves to Chinois Ibiza, where Bedouin open their SAGA summer residency. The opening night features Bedouin and a guest appearance by Luciano, as well as back-to-back sets from Jimi Jules & Tram and Manu R & Summer.

## Practical advice

Large crowds are expected in the port area. Visitors are advised to use public transport or taxis, as parking around the harbour is very limited in summer, and to bring water during the hot evenings.
