## Introduction to the Campaign
The Consell d'Eivissa, the island council of Ibiza, has marked the International Day for the Elimination of Violence Against Women with a call to action against masculine violence. The council has adhered to a manifesto by the Institut Balear de la Dona (IBDONA) and has released a video featuring institutional representatives and professionals from various fields, including the judiciary, healthcare, police, and social services, who have joined forces to condemn violence against women.

## The Manifesto and Video
The manifesto, read by Tonyi Ferrer of the Oficina de la Dona del Consell d'Eivissa, emphasizes the need for a collective response to masculine violence and encourages individuals to take action rather than remaining silent or indifferent. The video features various representatives, including the president of the Consell d'Eivissa, Vicent Marí, and professionals from different fields, who give voice to different fragments of the manifesto. The objective of the video is to convey a common message of rejection of any form of violence against women.

## Call to Action
The Consell d'Eivissa is calling on the entire society to get involved in the fight against masculine violence. According to the council, individuals can form part of the solution by listening, providing support, accompanying, rejecting masculine attitudes, or alerting the competent services in cases of violence. The council is committed to equality between men and women, prevention, and specialized attention to victims, as well as coordination between different administrations and services.

## Conclusion
As the manifesto states, "what is tolerated is perpetuated" and "silence and indifference protect the aggressors, never the victims." The Consell d'Eivissa is urging individuals to take responsibility and act against violence, emphasizing that "each gesture counts, each intervention matters, and each person can be part of the solution." The council's initiative aims to raise awareness and promote a collective response to eliminate violence against women.
