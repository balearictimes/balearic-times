Paris Saint-Germain, the reigning European champions, will play RCD Mallorca in this summer's Trofeu Ciutat de Palma, the city's traditional pre-season tournament.

The 48th edition, which this year also marks the 150th anniversary of sponsor Estrella Damm, will be played on 5 August at 8pm at the Estadi Mallorca Son Moix.

## Part of the Kang-in Lee deal

The fixture was agreed as part of the transfer that took South Korean midfielder Kang-in Lee from Mallorca to PSG. Lee, who made his name at Son Moix before moving to Paris, is expected to return to face his former club.

## Mallorca's first home game of the season

For Mallorca, the match will be the first home game of the 2026/27 campaign and their penultimate friendly before the league season begins. The club will play its final warm-up match on 8 August, against an opponent still to be confirmed.

The game gives manager and supporters a first look at how the squad, including any summer signings, measures up against one of the strongest sides in Europe.

## Tickets

Season-ticket holders will be able to attend free of charge. General sale tickets will be released later, with dates and prices to be announced through the club's official channels.

## A long-running tournament

The Trofeu Ciutat de Palma has been held since 1969 and has brought a long list of major European clubs to the island over the years. A visit from the Champions League holders makes this one of the most attractive editions in recent memory, and a large crowd is expected.
