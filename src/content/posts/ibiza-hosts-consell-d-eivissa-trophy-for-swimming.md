## Introduction to the Event
The Consell d'Eivissa is hosting the Trofeu Consell d'Eivissa de Fons i Estils, a swimming competition, at the des Raspallar pool this weekend. The event will take place on Saturday, 7 November, from 5:00 pm, and Sunday, 8 November, from 10:00 am. 

## Competition Details
The competition will feature swimmers from various categories, including aleví, infantil, júnior, and absolut, who will participate in 800 and 1,500 metres freestyle and 400 metres medley events. The 25-metre pool will be equipped with electronic timing. According to the Consell d'Eivissa, this trophy combines endurance and technique, allowing athletes and coaches to assess the swimmers' performance and progress during this part of the season.

## Organisers' Comments
The councillor of Presidency, Treasury, Economic Management, and Sports, Salvador Losa, highlighted the importance of continuing to host island competitions to support the work of local clubs and enable athletes to compete without leaving the island. He also emphasised the role of these events in the training of young athletes and in teaching values such as effort, perseverance, and teamwork. The director of Sports, Javier Bonet, noted that the combination of endurance and technique provides coaches with a useful reference to evaluate both the resistance and technical skills of participants and guide their training.

## Programme and Invitation
The competition programme includes various events on both Saturday and Sunday. The Consell d'Eivissa invites swimming fans to attend the event at the des Raspallar pool and support the island's swimmers. Trophies will be awarded to the winners of each category based on the sum of points from the two events. The schedule for the competition is as follows:
- Saturday, 7 November: 800 metres freestyle for women, 1,500 metres freestyle for women, and 400 metres medley for men
- Sunday, 8 November: 1,500 metres freestyle for men, 800 metres freestyle for men (aleví category), and 400 metres medley for women
