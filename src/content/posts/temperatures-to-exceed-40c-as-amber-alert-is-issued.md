Mallorca is facing a strong heatwave, with temperatures rising day by day towards a peak at the end of the week, when some inland areas could reach 43°C. The state meteorological agency, Aemet, has issued an amber warning for much of the island.

## Day by day

**Tuesday.** Highs close to 38°C inland and in the north and east of Mallorca. Menorca is under a yellow warning.

**Wednesday.** An amber warning comes into force for much of Mallorca, with highs of 39–40°C, especially in the north, north-east and east. The Serra de Tramuntana, the south of Mallorca and Menorca remain on yellow.

**Thursday.** Hotter still. Inland areas could pass 40°C, and nights will be very warm, with minimum temperatures above 25°C.

**Friday.** The likely peak, with some inland areas approaching 43°C. The amber warning remains.

## Wildfire risk

Aemet warns of a very high to extreme risk of wildfires, starting in the Migjorn area in the south of Mallorca and spreading across the island as the week goes on.

- Do not light fires or barbecues in the countryside.
- Never throw cigarette ends from cars or on the ground.
- Avoid using machinery that could cause sparks near dry vegetation.
- If you see smoke or flames, call 112 immediately.

## Staying safe in the heat

- Drink water regularly, even if you are not thirsty, and avoid alcohol in the hottest hours.
- Wear light, loose, light-coloured clothing and a hat.
- Plan outdoor activities, sport and walks for early morning or evening.
- Keep blinds closed during the day.
- Check on elderly neighbours and relatives, who are most at risk.

Dizziness, headache, nausea and extreme tiredness can be signs of heat exhaustion. Move to a cool place, drink water and rest; if symptoms worsen or the person becomes confused, call 112.
