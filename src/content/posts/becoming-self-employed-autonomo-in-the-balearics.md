Many people who work in the Balearic Islands run their own businesses as self-employed individuals, known in Spain as autónomos. They include bar owners, tradespeople, boat skippers, guides, therapists, photographers, consultants and people who let property. The legal route is simpler than forming a company, but it comes with obligations that new arrivals often underestimate. This guide follows the official guide to registering as an individual entrepreneur on ipyme.org, the Ministry of Inclusion and Social Security's information on the new contribution system, and the Agencia Tributaria's pages. It is general information rather than advice, and it was last checked on 29 October 2026.

## What being an autónomo means

An autónomo is a person who carries out an economic activity on their own account, without a company. The person is personally liable for debts, pays income tax on profits under the personal income tax system, and contributes to a special scheme of Social Security for self-employed workers. The scheme is known as RETA.

## Step 1: tax census registration

Before starting activity, you register with the Agencia Tributaria in the census of entrepreneurs, professionals and withholders, using form 036 or the simplified form 037. You mark your first activity and give the activity code, the business address and your contact details. You can file online with a digital certificate, in person at the tax office, or through a PAE advice point.

## Step 2: register with Social Security

You must register in RETA within 30 calendar days of starting. The application goes to the Tesorería General de la Seguridad Social, in person or through its electronic office. You choose your contribution, your mutual insurer and whether to cover accidents at work. You also set up a direct debit for payments.

## Step 3: use CIRCE if you prefer

The CIRCE system, the same electronic channel used to create companies, can file the paperwork for an individual entrepreneur through a single electronic document. It sends the information to Social Security and the tax authority. A PAE can help you complete it.

## Step 4: licences

Your town hall decides what permission your activity needs. It may be an activity licence, a responsible declaration or a prior communication. Some home-based professional activities with no public contact do not need a licence. Other sectors, such as food, security and gaming, require registration with their own authorities.

## How contributions work

Since 2023, self-employed workers contribute according to their net income, not a fixed base chosen at will. The Ministry of Inclusion explains that the system is being phased in with fifteen income brackets over several years, and that a definitive model based on real income must be completed within nine years of its start. The Ministry says that net income is calculated by taking expenses from income and then applying a generic deduction of 7 per cent, or 3 per cent for self-employed company partners.

For 2025, the Ministry's page gives a range from 200 euros a month at the minimum to 590 euros a month at the maximum. People whose net income is below the minimum wage pay between 200 and 260 euros a month. Those in the middle brackets pay about 290 to 294 euros. People earning more than 6,000 euros a month pay 590 euros. The figures for 2026 are set in the annual contributions order, Orden PJC/297/2026, published in the BOE, and a reader should check the table in that order for the current year.

You can change your contribution up to six times a year if you expect your income to change. At year end, the Social Security regularises contributions against your real net income from the tax return, which may produce a refund or a payment.

## The bonus for new starters

New self-employed workers can claim a reduced monthly contribution of 80 euros for twelve months. The Ministry says this can be extended for another twelve months if the person's net income remains low. Conditions apply, so check them before relying on the bonus.

## Taxes you must file

An autónomo pays income tax on profits. Most also have to charge VAT on their invoices and file quarterly VAT and income tax returns. The government guide notes that it does not cover these filings in detail, so check the Agencia Tributaria for the dates and the forms. Taxpayers who use the direct estimation method must keep accounting books and legalise them electronically within four months of the end of the year.

## If you hire staff

An autónomo with employees must register as an employer with Social Security before hiring, affiliate each worker, register contracts with the public employment service within 10 days, notify the regional labour authority of the workplace and display the work calendar. These steps are listed in the government guide, and the article on hiring staff covers them in more detail.

## If you are a foreign national

European Union citizens can register as self-employed in Spain with a NIE. Non-EU nationals, including British citizens, need a residence authorisation that allows self-employed work. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the immigration offices publish the requirements. A person who is working remotely for a foreign company has a different route, which is covered in the article on remote workers.

## Common mistakes

- Starting to trade before registering with the tax authority.
- Forgetting the 30-day deadline for Social Security.
- Not setting aside money for quarterly tax payments.
- Choosing a contribution that does not match expected income.
- Failing to keep invoices and receipts.

## Where to get help

The PAE points, local chambers of commerce, the Social Security offices and the Agencia Tributaria all provide information. Many autónomos use a gestoría to handle quarterly filings. Ask for a clear quote and check what is included.

## Questions people ask

**Can I register without a NIE?** Foreign nationals need a NIE for tax and Social Security registration. Get one before starting the process.

**Can I change my contribution later?** Yes, up to six times a year, as the Ministry explains, with a yearly adjustment against real net income.

**Do I need a gestor?** It is not required, but many self-employed workers use one to file quarterly returns.
