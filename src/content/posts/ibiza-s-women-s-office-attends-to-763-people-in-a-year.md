## Introduction to the Issue
The Consell d'Eivissa's Women's Office has attended to 763 people between November 2025 and October 2026, a 1.6% increase from the previous period. This rise in cases highlights the need for continued support and resources for women experiencing violence.

## Support Services for Victims
The Women's Office provides various services, including a 24-hour crisis centre called Espai Savina, which has attended to 82 women since its opening in March. The centre offers specialized attention and support for victims of sexual violence. According to the Consell d'Eivissa, the number of women seeking help for sexual violence has increased by 105% compared to the previous year.

The Women's Office also offers other programs, such as the service for attention and treatment of victims of gender violence, which has attended to 470 women. Of these, 267 women (56.8%) have suffered physical and psychological abuse, while 130 women (27.7%) have experienced psychological abuse. The office has also provided support to 102 people, including 67 women and 35 children, through its residential resources.

## Activities to Raise Awareness
To commemorate the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women on November 25, the Consell d'Eivissa has organized a program of activities under the slogan "No et quedis mirant. ACTUA davant la violència masclista" (Don't just stand there. Take action against sexist violence). The program includes a joint campaign with the Balearic Government, island councils, and town halls to raise awareness and promote prevention and training. The activities will continue throughout the year, with events such as film screenings, workshops, and exhibitions.

## Conclusion and Next Steps
The Consell d'Eivissa's efforts to support victims of violence and raise awareness about the issue are crucial in promoting a safer and more equal society. As Consellera Carolina Escandell emphasized, "the attention to victims does not end when the situation of violence stops. It is essential to accompany them throughout the recovery process, offer them psychological, legal, and social support, and help them recover their autonomy and life project." The Consell d'Eivissa will continue to work towards providing resources and support for those affected by violence, and the community is encouraged to participate in the activities and campaigns organized to prevent and eradicate sexist violence.
