Amnesia, one of Ibiza's most famous clubs, is marking its 50th anniversary with a free afternoon party on 8 July, with all donations going to environmental projects on the islands.

## A free party with a cause

Entry is free, but guests are encouraged to donate to Ibiza & Formentera Preservation, with all of the money going to conservation work in the Pitiusas.

## The headliner

The DJ for the event is Marlon Hoffstadt, the German producer who has become one of the most in-demand names in electronic music in recent years, with high-energy sets and a strong following among younger clubbers.

## Back to the beginning

Unlike Amnesia's usual all-night events, this party takes place in the afternoon, with a relaxed, open atmosphere. The idea is to evoke the club's early days in the 1970s, when it began in an old farmhouse near Sant Rafel and music was a meeting point for people of all ages, nationalities and backgrounds — long before Ibiza became a global party destination.

## Where the money goes

Ibiza & Formentera Preservation has been working for more than 15 years on the islands' main environmental challenges, supporting projects in:

- **marine conservation,** including the protection of Posidonia seagrass meadows;
- **land biodiversity,** protecting native plants and animals;
- **the circular economy,** reducing waste and promoting recycling;
- **sustainable local food production;**
- **research and monitoring** of environmental problems.

The foundation says it has helped raise more than €5 million for environmental projects since it was created.

## A message for the island

Tourism places heavy pressure on Ibiza's water, land and coastline. By linking its anniversary to conservation, Amnesia is sending a message that celebrating the island also means looking after it — and showing that club culture can contribute to that effort.

Half a century after it opened, Amnesia remains one of the defining names of Ibiza nightlife, and its anniversary party is a chance for residents as well as visitors to be part of that history.
