Spanish singer-songwriter Bebe returns to Ibiza after almost a decade with a concert at Las Dalias in Sant Carles on 9 July, as part of the venue's Las Dalias Live Music series.

## Two decades since Pafuera telarañas

The concert comes two decades after the release of Bebe's debut album, *Pafuera telarañas* (2004), one of the most influential Spanish records of its time. It included "Malo", "Ella" and "Siempre me quedará" — songs about freedom, identity, love and resilience. "Malo", a song about domestic violence, became an anthem and remains one of the most powerful protest songs in Spanish pop.

Since then, Bebe has continued to develop as an artist while keeping the raw honesty that won her a devoted following. Her live shows mix the emotional force of her best-known songs with the maturity of her later work.

## An intimate setting

The concert takes place on the Garden Stage at Las Dalias, a venue that has long been one of the centres of Ibiza's live music and alternative culture, famous for its hippy market. The setting allows a close connection between artist and audience.

## Practical information

- **Venue:** Las Dalias, Garden Stage, Sant Carles de Peralta
- **Date:** Thursday 9 July
- **Doors:** 8pm
- **Concert:** 9pm
- **Tickets:** through Las Dalias' website; early booking is recommended given the limited capacity

## Why it's special

- Bebe has not performed in Ibiza for almost ten years.
- The show celebrates the legacy of an album that marked a generation.
- Fans can expect new arrangements of familiar songs.

Las Dalias' summer programme has become one of the island's most interesting alternatives to the big club nights, bringing established Spanish and international artists to an intimate open-air stage.
