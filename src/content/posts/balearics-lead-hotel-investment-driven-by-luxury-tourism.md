The Balearic Islands attracted €577 million of hotel investment in the first half of 2026, more than any other region of Spain, as investors continued to bet on luxury tourism.

## The figures

- **€577 million** invested in Balearic hotels between January and June.
- **23%** of all hotel investment in Spain over the period.
- **€2.46 billion** invested in Spanish hotels in total, up 26.5% on the first half of 2025 — the highest figure ever recorded for the period.

## Where the money is going

Most of the money is being used to buy existing hotels rather than build new ones. Investors prefer to acquire established properties and upgrade them, focusing on four- and five-star hotels in well-known destinations.

Holiday hotels and luxury properties together account for around 60% of hotel investment in Spain.

## Record prices

Strong competition for the best assets has pushed the average price paid per hotel room in Spain to a record €213,300.

## Why the Balearics

- The islands combine strong international demand with a limited supply of new hotel licences, which makes existing hotels particularly valuable.
- Investors see the Balearics as a reliable market for premium tourism, with high occupancy and rising room rates.
- Many older hotels offer opportunities for renovation and repositioning upmarket.

## The debate

The investment boom reflects the islands' shift towards higher-spending tourism, a goal shared by the Balearic government and the hotel industry. Supporters argue that fewer, higher-spending visitors are better for the economy and the environment than mass tourism.

Critics warn that the trend is pushing up prices, making the islands less accessible to local people and to middle-income visitors, and that the rise of large investment funds in the hotel sector reduces the role of local family-owned businesses. Trade unions also point out that the benefits of luxury tourism need to reach workers through better pay and conditions.

## The outlook

With demand for premium holidays remaining strong, analysts expect investment in Balearic hotels to continue — although the limited number of properties available for sale may restrict how much more money can flow in.
