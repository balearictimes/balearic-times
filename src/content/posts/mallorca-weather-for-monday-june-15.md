Monday could bring something unusual to Mallorca: Aemet says there is a chance of "mud rain" — light showers carrying Saharan dust — and even a dry thunderstorm, although most forecasts show only a small chance of rain. Skies will be cloudy at times, temperatures similar to Sunday's and breezy in many areas.

## The week ahead

After Monday, the weather turns sunny with occasional cloud, and temperatures will climb through the week, reaching around 38°C inland on Saturday.

## Sunday's readings

The highest temperature on Sunday afternoon was 33.7°C in Porreres. The coolest overnight reading was 9.4°C at Son Torrella in the Tramuntana mountains.

## What is mud rain?

When southerly winds carry dust from the Sahara over the Mediterranean, rain falling through the dusty air brings the particles down with it, leaving a layer of brownish mud on cars, terraces and windows. It is harmless in itself, but can be a nuisance. Dust in the air can also affect air quality, so people with respiratory problems should take care.

## Tips

- If you were planning to wash your car, wait until after Monday.
- Keep a light jacket for breezy areas.
- With temperatures rising later in the week, start getting into hot-weather habits: water, shade and avoiding the hottest hours.
