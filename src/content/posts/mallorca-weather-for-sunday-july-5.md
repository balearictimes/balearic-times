A heatwave is arriving in Mallorca. The State Meteorological Agency (AEMET) has issued yellow heat alerts for the south and the interior of the island on Sunday 5 July, from midday to 7pm, with highs of around 37°C. The episode is expected to last at least until midweek.

## Alert levels in Mallorca

- **Yellow:** issued when maximums are expected to reach around 36–37°C, depending on the zone.
- **Amber:** from around 39°C.
- **Red:** reserved for extreme heat of around 42–43°C or more.

Humidity will be very low inland, around 15%.

## The coming days

From Monday to Wednesday, highs will be in the mid-30s across much of the island, with inland areas around Binissalem approaching 40°C by Wednesday. Coastal areas will be a few degrees cooler, but still hot.

## Saturday's figures

Saturday's maximums ranged from 33.2°C at Palma airport to 35.2°C in Llucmajor. Inland, overnight minimums were between 15°C and 18°C — a relief that is likely to disappear as the heat builds.

## Staying safe

- **Drink water** regularly, even if you're not thirsty, and more if you are active.
- **Dress** in light, loose, light-coloured clothing and wear a hat and sunglasses.
- **Plan** exercise, beach trips and outdoor work for early morning or evening.
- **Keep cool** at home: close blinds during the day and ventilate at night.
- **Look out** for others, especially older people living alone, babies and people with chronic illnesses.

Dizziness, headache, heavy sweating, nausea or a racing pulse can be signs of heat exhaustion. Move to a cool place, drink water and seek medical help if symptoms do not improve. In an emergency, call 112.
