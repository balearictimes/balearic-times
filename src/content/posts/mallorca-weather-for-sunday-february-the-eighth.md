After a calm Saturday, Mallorca faces an unsettled start to Sunday, with Aemet warning of possible thunderstorms before dawn and rough seas along the south and east coasts.

## Sunday's warnings

A yellow warning for coastal conditions is in force for the south and east of the island until midday on Sunday, with:

- waves of 3–4 metres;
- wind gusts of around 60 km/h;
- difficult conditions for boats and on exposed coastal paths.

Skies will be mostly cloudy, with gusts of 55–60 km/h inland.

## The week ahead

Monday is expected to be free of warnings, and the rest of the week looks drier, sunnier and milder:

- little risk of rain through Friday;
- temperatures rising into the low 20s;
- winds strengthening again on Thursday and Friday, with gusts of 55–65 km/h.

## Saturday's readings

The warmest places on Saturday were Son Servera (18.9°C), Muro (18.0°C) and Capdepera (17.8°C). The coldest overnight readings were at Son Torrella in Escorca (5.8°C) and Lluc (9.0°C). The strongest gusts were 82 km/h at Alfàbia in the Tramuntana, 77 km/h on Cabrera and 64 km/h at Portocolom.

## Advice

- Stay away from sea walls and exposed rocks while waves are high.
- Boat owners should check moorings and postpone trips until the warning ends.
- Walkers on coastal paths should take extra care in the wind.

For official warnings, check Aemet's website or app.
