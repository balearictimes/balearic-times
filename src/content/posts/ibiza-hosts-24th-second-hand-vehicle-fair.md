## Introduction to the Fair
The Consell d'Eivissa has announced that the 24th Saló de Vehicles d’Ocasió, a second-hand vehicle fair, will take place on the weekend of November 6-8 at the Recinte Firal d’Eivissa. The fair, which has become a consolidated event, will feature six Ibiza-based companies from the automotive sector: Grupauto Pitiusas, LC Group Rental Car SL, Class Rent a Car, Tanit Automoción, Punicauto, and Proa Ocasión.

## What to Expect
According to the Consell d'Eivissa, the fair will offer a wide range of vehicles, including utility vehicles, high-end models, and industrial vehicles. Visitors will be able to compare different options and receive direct advice from the participating companies. The consellera de Promoció Econòmica, Maria Fajarnés, said that "this fair is an opportunity to give visibility to the island's automotive companies and bring their offer closer to those seeking a second-hand vehicle with the advice and guarantee of professionals from the island".

## Participating Companies and Fair Details
Miguel Ángel Escandell, president of the Associació de Tallers de la PIMEEF, explained that there will be a wide range of vehicles on offer, including utility vehicles, cars, commercial vehicles, sports cars, and high-end models. He encouraged everyone to participate, as it will be an opportunity to discover interesting offers with direct discounts from the exhibitors. The fair will be open from 10:30 am to 9:00 pm on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday, with free entry, parking facilities, and a cafeteria service available throughout the three days.

## Conclusion
The 24th Saló de Vehicles d’Ocasió aims to promote the island's automotive companies and provide a platform for people to find a second-hand vehicle with the advice and guarantee of professionals from the island. As Fajarnés stated, "after twenty-four editions, this fair has become a meeting point between companies and potential buyers". The Consell d'Eivissa will continue to support initiatives that generate economic activity and support the island's business fabric.
