## Introduction to the Campaign
The Consell d'Eivissa, Ibiza's island council, has launched a new audiovisual campaign called 'El buit que queda' (The Void That Remains) to raise awareness about the devastating consequences of gender violence. The campaign, presented at the IES Quartó de Portmany school in Sant Antoni, features a video created by students of the Batxillerat d'Arts Escèniques (Baccalaureate of Performing Arts) and is part of the council's efforts to mark the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women on November 25.

## The Video and Its Message
The video shows the void left by a woman who has been a victim of gender violence, told through four different perspectives: her children, colleagues, parents, and healthcare professionals. The story is connected by a symbolic element, a toy that represents a mother and child, and invites viewers to reflect on the human and social consequences of violence against women. According to the Consell d'Eivissa, the campaign aims to sensitize society, especially young people, to the impact of gender violence on families and communities.

## The Importance of Youth Participation
The campaign has been promoted by the Department of Social Welfare, Family and Equality of the Consell d'Eivissa, in collaboration with the Centre d'Estudi i Prevenció de Conductes Addictives (CEPCA). The consellera of Social Welfare, Family and Equality, Carolina Escandell, highlighted the importance of involving young people in the prevention of gender violence and thanked the students who participated in the project. "With this campaign, we want to remind people that behind every woman who is killed, there is a life cut short, but also children, parents, friends, and colleagues who have to live with an irreparable absence," she said.

## Future Plans and Previous Initiatives
The project is part of a series of initiatives by the Consell d'Eivissa, CEPCA, and IES Quartó de Portmany to create awareness campaigns featuring young people and targeting this demographic. Previous projects include campaigns against gambling addiction, promoting coexistence, and preventing violence against women through social media. The Consell d'Eivissa has once again chosen to use audiovisual language and active youth participation as tools to promote reflection, equality, and collective commitment against gender violence. The campaign will be broadcast on social media and in cinemas, and is expected to contribute to a greater awareness and understanding of the issue among young people in Ibiza.
