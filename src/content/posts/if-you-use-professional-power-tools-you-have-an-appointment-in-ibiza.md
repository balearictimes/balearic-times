Professional tradespeople and keen DIYers in Ibiza can try out the latest DeWALT power tools at a free two-day event hosted by Servicios Palau, a long-standing distributor on the island, on 10 and 11 February.

## Hands-on demonstrations

DeWALT technicians will run live demonstrations of the brand's latest professional equipment, focusing on:

- new battery technology, including the PowerStack range;
- durability features designed for tough building-site conditions;
- connectivity options for tracking and managing tools.

The emphasis is on letting professionals test tools under realistic working conditions and ask questions directly of the manufacturer's specialists.

## The ranges on show

DeWALT, founded in the United States in 1924, will be showing:

- its 20V MAX range of cordless tools;
- FLEXVOLT batteries, which switch voltage automatically depending on the tool;
- TOUGHSYSTEM storage and transport cases.

## A long partnership

Servicios Palau has worked with DeWALT for around 25 years, supplying builders, installers and other trades in the Pitiusas.

## Practical details

- **Dates:** 10 and 11 February
- **Time:** 9am to 6pm
- **Place:** Servicios Palau, Polígono Industrial Sa Coma, Ibiza
- **Entry:** free; professionals can register on the day

## Why it matters

Construction and building maintenance are a major part of Ibiza's economy, particularly over the winter, when hotels, villas and restaurants carry out renovations ahead of the summer season. Events like this give local professionals the chance to try new equipment before investing in it, without having to travel to the mainland.

## Tips before buying cordless tools

- **Stick to one battery platform** where possible, so batteries can be shared between tools.
- **Check the warranty** and the availability of local servicing and spare parts.
- **Consider the total cost**, including batteries and chargers, not just the tool itself.
- **Look at safety features**, such as brakes and kickback protection on saws and grinders.
