Skaters will have the chance to race on the same asphalt as Formula 1 and MotoGP stars at the Graviteo Skate Race, held at the Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya on 19 July as part of the Graviteo Urban Sports Festival.

## The race

- **Date:** Sunday 19 July
- **Start:** 10am (bib collection from 9am)
- **Distances:** 5 km, ideal for beginners, and 10 km
- **Categories:** under-11, under-15 and absolute, each with men's and women's divisions

## The circuit

The track is 4,727 metres long, with a 1,047-metre main straight, a width of 11–12 metres, nine right-hand and five left-hand bends, a maximum gradient of 6% and a total elevation change of almost 30 metres. Riding the same corners as the world's top drivers and riders is a big part of the appeal.

## Safety

Helmets are compulsory for all participants. The finish area will be divided into two lanes with cones to separate faster and slower skaters.

## Prizes

- **Under-11 and under-15:** medals for the top three in the 10 km race.
- **Absolute 10 km:** prize money for the top five.

| Position | Prize |
|---|---|
| 1st | €600 |
| 2nd | €400 |
| 3rd | €250 |
| 4th | €150 |
| 5th | €100 |

The first 1,000 people to register will receive an official event T-shirt.

## Who can take part

The race is open to skaters of all levels, from children and families looking for a fun day out to experienced skaters aiming for a podium. As in a popular marathon, everyone can set their own goal.

## Registration

Registration is online through the festival's website. Signing up early guarantees a place and the free T-shirt.

For skaters from the Balearics, the event offers a rare opportunity: there is no circuit of this kind on the islands, and skating a full Formula 1 lap is an experience few will forget.
