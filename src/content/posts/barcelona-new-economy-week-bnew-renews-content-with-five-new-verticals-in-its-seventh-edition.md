Barcelona New Economy Week (BNEW), the business and innovation event organised by the Consorci de la Zona Franca de Barcelona, will hold its seventh edition from 5 to 7 October 2026 at DFactory Barcelona, with a refreshed programme that includes five new thematic areas.

## Hybrid again

BNEW will keep its hybrid format. All talks and panels will be streamed live on a digital platform, so people can follow them from anywhere, while those attending in person will have dedicated spaces for networking.

## Eight sectors

This year's programme is organised around eight areas of the new economy:

- aviation and space;
- agri-food;
- gaming;
- talent;
- science;
- health;
- artificial intelligence;
- energy.

## On the agenda

Among the issues to be discussed:

- how artificial intelligence is changing work and the economy;
- interdisciplinary science and open innovation;
- new materials, nanotechnology and space research;
- the energy transition and the use of data to improve efficiency;
- how geopolitical change is affecting the aerospace and defence industry;
- automation and surgical robotics in healthcare;
- smart water management and the future of food;
- the business of video games, from financing to launches.

## Speakers and activities

Around 270 Spanish and international experts will take part in more than 50 panels, debates and sessions, alongside more than 30 hands-on activities showcasing developments in each sector.

## Networking

A personalised online platform will allow participants to message each other, exchange contacts and book meetings, and a virtual assistant will answer questions about the programme.

## Why it matters

BNEW is part of the Free Zone Consortium's strategy to position Barcelona as a hub for innovation and new industries, from start-ups to advanced manufacturing. DFactory, the consortium's industry 4.0 centre in the Zona Franca, hosts companies working in areas such as robotics, 3D printing and artificial intelligence, making it a fitting venue for an event focused on the economy of the future.
