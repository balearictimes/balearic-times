## Introduction to Inclusive Sport
The Consell d'Eivissa, through its Department of Sports, has organised a new event as part of the sixth edition of the educational programme "Esport Inclusiu a l'Escola" (Inclusive Sport at School). The programme aims to promote inclusive sport in schools on the island and foster values such as equality of opportunities, respect for diversity, cooperation, and personal improvement.

## Meeting the Athlete
110 students from the CC Mare de Déu de les Neus school had the opportunity to meet Ignacio Arribas, a ten-time Spanish swimming champion and record holder in the S10 category. Arribas shared his personal and sporting experience with the students, talking about the challenges he has faced throughout his career and the importance of effort, consistency, and personal improvement.

## Practical Workshops
After the talk, the students participated in practical workshops on the school's sports pitches, where they could try out different inclusive sports such as adapted football for people with visual and physical disabilities, sitting volleyball, goalball, and athletics for people with physical and visual disabilities. The activities were supported by the programme's technicians, the school's teachers, and Arribas himself, who led the sitting volleyball workshop.

## Promoting Inclusive Sport
The "Esport Inclusiu a l'Escola" programme is promoted and funded by the Consell d'Eivissa's Department of Sports and co-organised with the Cátedra Fundación Sanitas de Estudios sobre Deporte Inclusivo (CEDI). The programme aims to bring inclusive sport to the island's schools and promote values such as equality and respect for diversity among young people. According to the Consell d'Eivissa, the programme is an important initiative to promote inclusive sport and make it accessible to all students, regardless of their abilities.
