A yellow heat warning is in force for the south of Mallorca on Monday from midday to 8pm, with temperatures expected to peak around 36°C. The day will be mostly sunny and breezy at times. After that, the heat should ease slightly, and further warnings look less likely this week.

## What a yellow warning means

A yellow warning indicates that temperatures are high enough to pose a risk to people who are vulnerable or active outdoors, although they are not unusual for the time of year. It is a reminder to drink plenty of water, seek shade and avoid strenuous activity in the hottest hours.

Breezes will be mainly from the east and north-east, moderate at times.

## The rest of the week

- From Tuesday, maximum temperatures should drop by a couple of degrees.
- Winds will come mainly from the north, keeping things a little fresher.
- There is a chance of showers on Wednesday or Thursday.

## Sunday's readings

Sunday was one of the hottest days of the season so far, with 37.9°C in Binissalem, 37.8°C in Santa Maria and 37.2°C at Palma airport.

## Tips for the heat

- Carry water and drink regularly.
- Wear light-coloured, loose clothing and a hat.
- Plan sport and beach trips for the morning or evening.
- Apply sun cream of SPF 30 or more before going out and reapply every two hours.
- If you feel dizzy, unusually tired or have a headache, move to the shade, drink water and rest.
